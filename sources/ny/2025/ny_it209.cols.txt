The noncustodial parent New York State earned income credit (noncustodial EIC) may be claimed instead of the New York State earned income credit (NYS EIC). If you claimed a federal earned income credit, calculate both the noncustodial EIC (Schedule A) and the NYS EIC (Schedule B) on Form IT-209 to determine which credit is more beneficial to you. You cannot claim both the noncustodial EIC and the NYS EIC.
