Comment on tax forms and publications
Only tips received in an occupation that customarily and regularly received tips on or before Dec. 31, 2024 (List of Occupations that Receive Tips) are considered qualified tips that be can be used to figure the amount of the No Tax on Tips deduction on Form 1040, Schedule 1-A. Use the List of Occupations that Received Tips to determine if the tips you received are qualified tips.
