                                                                        Introduction
                                                                        The purpose of this publication is to provide information
                                                                        on figuring and claiming the deduction for business use of
                                                                        your home. The term “home” includes a house, apartment,
                                                                        condominium, mobile home, boat, or similar property that
                                                                        provides basic living accommodations. It also includes
                                                                        structures on the property, such as an unattached garage,
                                                                        studio, barn, or greenhouse. However, it does not include
                                                                        any part of your property used exclusively as a hotel, mo-
                                                                        tel, inn, or similar establishment.
