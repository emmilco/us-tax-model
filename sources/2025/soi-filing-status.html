A complete list of tables from various sources and publications which are classified by filing (marital) status.

The tables are available as Microsoft Excel® files. A free Excel viewer is available for download, if needed.

The tables are grouped into the following categories:

  • Individual income tax returns filed and sources of income
  • Individual income tax returns with exemptions and itemized deductions
  • Individual income tax returns with tax computation
  • Returns with salaries and wages reported on Forms W-2
  • Individual income tax: Income by gender and age from information returns
  • Taxpayers with IRA plans
