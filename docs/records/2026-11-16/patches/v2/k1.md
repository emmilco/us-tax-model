## 3. Wording judgment (protocol section 2)

- **Spouse phrase.** The README rule "exactly one phrase" covers questions asked once per return. It does not cover a
  question asked per roster entry [C interview/README.md:638-652], and K-1 questions are per entry, so a different
  phrase is allowed. It is also needed. The stored "(or, on a joint return, your spouse)" is shown to a married-filing-
  separately filer as plain "you" [C README.md:661-667; engine/src/spouse-wording.ts], so today the spouse disappears
  exactly where the bug bites. The new phrase is not a near-miss under the build's regex [S: the regex from
  spouse-wording.ts:241 run over every changed text], and it is shown as stored on every return. On a single return
  it reads slightly odd, but it is correct, and it also covers a married head of household (whose spouse's
  participation counts) [I].
- **Spouse in active participation** (`decisions`, `k1s.active`): p925 p. 5, "If you or your spouse actively
  participated" [C sources/2025/p925.txt:355-356]. In the 10% test (`tenth`) the spouse's interest is included [C
  i8582.txt:227-229].
- **Who could answer No wrongly** (protocol 2.3):
  - UPE: a partner who was repaid in part. The prompt says "without being paid back".
  - Loan interest: a partner who borrowed against a home to buy in. The help says "A loan used to buy your share".
  - Dwelling: a partner whose relative stayed. The help names family and below-fair-rent use (p527 p. 26).
  - Real estate professional on a separate (MFS) return: "(or your spouse)" counts a spouse who should not count, so
    the error goes only toward refusal. The red team agreed [C wording-redteam.md:320-321].
  - The dwelling box says "over 14 days" and leaves out the 10% branch. A filer at 15-36 days may tick it: a refusal
    the law would not require (over-refusal only). The full test is in the prompt.
- **N10 (prompt including frame; aim under 40 for a child).** Still over: `k1p.t6` 45 (was 36, already over; the
  interview lane noted it), `k1s.mp_other` 44 (was 38), `k1p.dwelling` 38. The others are under 40 or grew by the
  phrase's 2 to 9 words. On a tick screen only the box label is shown; the prompt is behind "What this asks".
- **Steps (N12).** A K-1 with any box shown gains the `k1p_carry` screen (one step) and, when box 2 or 3 shows an
  amount, the `k1p_rental` screen. An S corporation K-1 gains `k1s_activity` (one step). Nothing is reachable today,
  because `scope.k1` refuses at the scope question.
