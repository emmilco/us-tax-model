## N10 exceedances among new questions

**Limits (protocol N10):** a prompt under 25 words in a parent and under 40 in a child; help at most about 40 words.

**What was checked:** all 300 question ids that are in AFTER's bank and not in BEFORE's. Prompts were taken from the
built `canonical/2025/interview-full.json`, including the "For this … —" entry prefix the page shows.

**Classification:**
- A parent is a tick-box member, a `scope.*` question or `f1040.digital_assets`; every other question is treated as a
  child. Some "children" that open further questions (for example `rental.personal_any`) are really parents, so the
  child limit is the lenient reading for them.
- A tick-box member shows only its label on the screen (5 to 12 words); its full prompt sits behind the disclosure.
  Whether the 25-word parent limit applies to that hidden prompt is a judgment the owner should make.

**What the counts cannot show:**
- Changes to the wording of existing questions are not included.
- Counts are of the bank's own wording; a single filer's version may drop "(or, on a joint return, your spouse)".
