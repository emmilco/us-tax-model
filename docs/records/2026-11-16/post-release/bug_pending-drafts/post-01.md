## BUG-NEW-post-01 — rec: two state and local refunds can put more on Schedule 1 than the taxes deducted, when the 2024 limit on taxes did not apply

- **Status:** draft, not filed. A lead from lane M-rental, grounded here against the form and the archived text but not run. The reading of footnote 1 needs the owner or a judgment lane before the entry is filed with `new_bug.py --pending`.
- **Caught by:** lane M-rental (claude-opus-5-5, judgment), 2026-11-16, while writing the fixture `mrental.rec.w2a_refunds_over_5d` (`round7/mrental.md:179-189`, "One lead for the owner, not drafted"). Model and source read by lane POST (claude-fable-5-2, judgment), 2026-11-16.
- **Source:** Pub. 525 (2025) p. 26, Worksheet 2a, line 3 ("Total state and local refunds. Add lines 1 and 2. But don't enter more than the amount of your state and local taxes shown on your 2024 Schedule A (Form 1040), line 5d"), line 4 ("No. Enter the amount from line 3 on line 4 and go to line 6"), line 7 ("If line 5 is blank, enter the amount from line 1") and line 8 ("If line 5 is blank, enter the amount from line 2"); p. 27, Worksheet 2, footnote 1 ("Don't enter more than the amount deducted for the prior year. Don't enter more than the excess of your state and local income tax deduction over your state and local general sales taxes you could have deducted"); p. 26, Recovery limited to deduction ("The amount you include in your income is limited to the smaller of: • The amount deducted, or • The amount recovered"). Text read in `sources/2025/p525.txt:2529-2560` and `2623-2625` on the xint worktree; the page numbers are the ones the form's own cites give and were not re-checked against the PDF.
- **Symptom:** inputs of the fixture: an income tax refund of $3,000, a real estate tax refund of $2,000, 2024 Schedule A line 5d = line 5e = $4,000 (the limit on taxes did not apply). Worksheet 2a line 3 is capped at $4,000, but on the "No" branch of line 4 line 5 stays blank, so lines 7 and 8 take lines 1 and 2 as entered: Worksheet 2 line 1a = $3,000 and line 1b = $2,000. The model reports $3,000 on Schedule 1 line 1 and $2,000 on line 8z (as M-rental observed; not re-run here). That is $5,000 of recoveries against $4,000 of taxes deducted. Footnote 1 and the "limited to the smaller of the amount deducted or the amount recovered" rule cap the two lines at what was deducted. The model follows the worksheet's lines word for word and applies no footnote 1 cap; its cites for lines 1a and 1b do not quote footnote 1, while the other recovery lines quote footnote 2. Effect: income is overstated, against the filer. The later lines of Worksheet 2 (the excess of itemized deductions over the standard deduction, and the taxable-income test) can lower the total again, so the overstatement does not always reach the return.
- **Where:** forms/2025/rec.yaml on the xint worktree: `rec.w2a_line1` (606-629, copies `refund.received_if_worksheet` with no cap), `rec.w2a_line2` (630-640), `rec.w2a_line3` (653-666, the only cap at line 5d), `rec.w2a_line7` (768-790, takes `rec.w2a_line1` when `rec.w2a_cap` is false), `rec.w2a_line8` (830-852, the same for line 2), `rec.line1a` (853-863), `rec.line1b` (864-873). For comparison, the Form 1040 instructions' worksheet path does cap the refund: `refund.line1` in forms/2025/refund.yaml (403-418) is the smaller of the refund and line 5d, under `amb.2025.refund_cap_5d`. The input `rec.property_tax` (267-279) says "taxes you deducted for 2024", which asks the filer to apply the limit; `refund.received` carries no such limit on this path.
- **When it arises:** only when the refunds entered exceed the 2024 taxes deducted and the 2024 limit did not apply (line 5d not more than line 5e). That needs a refund larger than the tax of that kind deducted, for example a refund that includes a refundable state credit, or a 2025 refund of a year other than 2024 entered here by mistake. Rare (inferred; no count made).
- **Proposed fix:** (pending the reading) on the branch where line 5 is blank, limit lines 7 and 8 so that 1a + 1b is not more than Worksheet 2a line 3, dividing line 3 between them in the ratio of line 1 to line 2, which is how the worksheet itself divides line 5. The worksheet does not say how to divide the cap on this branch, so the choice needs a register entry beside `amb.2025.refund_cap_5d`. Add the footnote 1 quote to the cites of `rec.line1a` and `rec.line1b`. The second sentence of footnote 1 (the sales-tax excess) is handled by `rec.sales_tax` and is not part of this entry; check that when fixing. Add a value fixture: the M-rental case asserts only 2a lines 3, 4 and 5 and so does not pin the outcome either way.
- **What would change the reading:** a finding that footnote 1 is addressed to the filer's own entry and that the model's input wording already carries it (it does for the property tax refund, loosely; it does not for the income tax refund on this path); or an IRS example with refunds above the deduction on the "No" branch.
- **Severity:** medium by the value rule (a wrong amount on a computed return), with small reach; low if the owner reads the cap as the filer's duty at the input. The coordinator decides at filing.
