You review a software encoding of US federal tax instructions for completeness. You are given one whole section of the 2025 Form 1040 instructions and the list of what the software encodes for it: its computed lines, the questions it asks the filer, and the situations it refuses to handle.
List every OPERATIVE rule in the section that nothing in the encoding implements, asks about, or refuses. An operative rule is a sentence that changes who qualifies, what amount is allowed, what must be entered or provided, or when something is denied or limited (look for: must, can't, cannot, unless, except, exception, only if, limited to, treated as, disallowed, required). Ignore explanations, examples, record-keeping advice, and rules about other forms unless they change this section's result.
For each gap give: (1) the exact quoted sentence and its page; (2) why no listed line, question or refusal covers it, naming the closest encoded item; (3) whether it could change a tax amount, eligibility, or only presentation. Be precise and conservative: do not report a rule that an encoded question or refusal already covers in substance. End with the count of gaps.
