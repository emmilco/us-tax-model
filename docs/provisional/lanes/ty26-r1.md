## What changed

- `years.json`: `"ny_adopted": [2025]` beside `adopted`.
- `tools/taxyear.py`: `adopted_years(key)` reads either list and exits on a missing or malformed one.
- `tools/jurisdiction.py`: `ny_status(year)` returns (checked, reason). A year is checked when `ny_adopted` lists it. A
  listed year without `forms/ny/<year>/` exits. For a year that is not listed but has `forms/ny/<year>/`, the reason
  says to add it to `ny_adopted`. The CLI `--ny-status [--year N]` exits 0 (checked), 3 (skipped, reason printed) or
  2 (bad list); `--self-test` covers 7 cases.
- `tools/check_all_steps.sh`: asks `jurisdiction.py --ny-status` once. For a year New York has not adopted, every
  `ny_*` step is skipped and not started. Its wave lists it as `== ny_<name>` / `skipped: <reason>`, it is left out of
  `step_output.py` and the times file, and the run prints the reason at its start and the count at its end. The New
  York render runs only when `forms/ny/<year>/` exists, and otherwise says it skipped. When New York has adopted the
  year, every step runs exactly as before.
- `engine/src/build.ts`: `dropNyConflicts()`. Without `forms/ny/<year>/`, conflict checks keyed `ny_*` are neither
  checked nor written to `conflicts.json`, and the build's output says so:
  `conflict checks: 1 naming a New York gate skipped (ny_it201.gate.line79_over): no forms/ny/2026/`. Two cases were
  added to `check-self-test` (`engine/src/check-selftest.ts`).
- `tools/check_year_ids.py`: refuses a year whose `canonical/<year>/` is missing or holds no document. Before this, a
  year with no build passed on zero ids (T-295). Two cases were added to the self-test.
- New `tools/test_check_all_ny_skip.sh`: runs the real steps script, `taxyear.py` and `jurisdiction.py` in a
  temporary repository whose other tools, `node` and `npm` are stubs that log their arguments. It covers an adopted
  year, a year not adopted (with and without `forms/ny/<year>/`), a year adopted with its data, and a listed year
  without data. With the skip disabled it fails with 8 problems.
- `tools/self_tests.py` gains two rows (`jurisdiction_self_test`, `check_all_ny_skip_self_test`), and
  `tools/README.md` ("New York years") and the `years.json` row of `PROJECT_INDEX.md` describe the list. These three
  files are outside the scope's file list.

## Decisions

- **The skip key is the explicit list, not the presence of data.** The plan chose this for D1. A catalog exists
  before the data builds, so `tools/sources/ny/<year>.json` is not a reliable sign of an adopted year.
- **`ny_adopted` need not be a subset of `adopted`.** This lets the New York checks of a year under preparation run
  through `--year` before either jurisdiction's year is adopted. A listed year must have `forms/ny/<year>/`.
- **The build's criterion is `forms/ny/<year>/`, as the plan says. The New York render uses the same criterion,** which
  keeps it consistent with `build_fresh.unrendered`.
- **Required self-test lines are not demanded of skipped steps** (`ny_compare` and `ny_mutate` would otherwise fail
  `step_output`).
