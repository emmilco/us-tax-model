## BUG-0593 (refund): real

Checked against i1040gi pp. 88-90 and p525 p. 24. The worksheet tests a refund against one 2024 return (lines 1, 2, 4
and 5 name "your 2024 Schedule A" and "the filing status claimed on your 2024 Form 1040"); the Exception's nine items do
not name a joint 2025 return after two separate 2024 returns (item 9 is the reverse case). Neither source prints a
per-spouse method. So I built the gate, not the per-spouse worksheet (that would be arithmetic the instructions do not
print and doubles the worksheet's questions); the choice is recorded in `amb.2025.refund_two_returns` for the owner.

Model (forms/2025/refund.yaml):
- `refund.joint` (f1040.status = mfj), `refund.no` (param sa.no).
- `refund.two_returns` (asked on a joint return, gate `refund.two_returns_if_joint`): "Were these refunds for 2024 tax
  of both of you, when each of you filed your own 2024 federal return (for example, before you married, or as married
  filing separately)?" Help: "If only one of you got a refund, answer No; the questions that follow are then about that
  person's own 2024 return."
- `refund.two_itemized` (asked on Yes, gate `refund.two_itemized_if_two`): "Did either of you itemize deductions on your
  own 2024 federal return and deduct state and local income taxes there (not general sales taxes)?" Help says a No means
  none is taxed and a Yes ends the interview.
- `refund.worksheet` is now a choose: false when `refund.two_returns_if_joint`, else `refund.worksheet_one` (the old
  test). So two returns with neither itemizing give zero (the p. 88 TIP holds whichever return is meant).
- `refund.taxable` refuses on `refund.two_itemized_if_two` (form "Pub. 525, Itemized Deduction Recoveries (Worksheet
  2)"), listed after box 8 and refund.not_prior_year.
- Asking order: seq 5 and 6 of order 165, after the year questions and before refund.itemized.

Who could answer No wrongly: a couple who filed separate federal 2024 returns but one joint state return. The words
"2024 tax of both of you" lead them to Yes. A couple who filed one joint 2024 return answers No at once. Cost for the
typical couple with a refund is one question (25 words) with an obvious No. Nobody else is asked (single filers, and
couples with no refund). No persona has a state refund, so no persona walk changes in steps (inferred from the gating,
not walked).
