## Decisions

- **Years of birth are listed, not generated from the parameter and not moved automatically.** No label, source, help
  or prompt is templated today; templating would reach the build, the renderer, the interview runtime and the literal
  comparison of labels with printed forms, for about 65 lines that the list now hands over with the parameter named
  and the moved text ready. Automatic moving would be wrong on fixed birth dates that share the same years (Form
  4972's 1936, the 2001 election, a 1976 gift, September 11, 2001).
- **Adjacent years are listed with a candidate, not moved**: some are statutory dates (car loans "originated after
  December 31, 2024").
- **Shared files: an adoption-time list, not per-year files or a silent skip.** Generators, pins and adapters are code;
  a per-year copy of code is not maintainable, and a skip of ids the year lacks would hide renames as quietly falling
  coverage. A shared file serves both years, so each needs a judgment the list lets the adoption lane make. The list
  does not refuse adoption; making a non-empty list fail is the coordinator's call.
- An `age.*` Count with no `moves_with_year` is taken to move by 1, so the rule works before and after P2's flags.
