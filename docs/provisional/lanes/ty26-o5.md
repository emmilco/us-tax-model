## Decisions
- **Where the filer test sits.** The return-level filer test is in `f8863.eligible`, which zeroes lines 1 and 10. It
  is repeated per student on the person side so that the twin structure is the same on both sides. The repetition is
  harmless.
- **Reused lines.** `household.any_work_ssn` and `dependent.work_ssn_known` are reused. They are existing lines whose
  definitions match i8863's "valid SSN" (valid for employment, issued before the due date), so no new input was
  needed.
- **Year literals.** Years are rolled only on the lines that the assigned entries name, plus the rest of Form 8880
  (C-CRED-16 is "Form 8880 year roll"). Other Part III year literals (`edu_inst`, `edu_a_this_year`, `edu_line23`,
  `_line25`, `_line26`, the worksheet citations, `f8863.claimed_count`/`none_claimed`) are left to adoption's literal
  list.
- **Change ids.** C-CRED-18 and C-PEOP-16 describe one rule, so they share one entry, `C-CRED-18+C-PEOP-16`, in the
  sa.yaml style. C-CRED-17 (f8863 lines 3 and 14) is lane W1's and is not touched.
- **C-CRED-15.** The citations that quote the amounts (`f8880.past9`, `f8880.can_take`) are left to the parameter
  lane.
