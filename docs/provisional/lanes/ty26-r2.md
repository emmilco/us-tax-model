# Lane R2: carry what adoption drops (TY2026 wave 1)

Model claude-opus-5-5, judgment; year 2026, jurisdiction us (the carry works for both). Inputs: T-295, the New York
rehearsal record `docs/ny/2026-11-14_ny_year_handling.md` (steps 18, 22, 24), BUG-0652.

## What changed

- `tools/year_diff.py --adopt` now carries, never over an existing file:
  - the citation rulings, as before (marked "carried from <year>");
  - the coverage rulings and baselines, byte for byte: `operands`, `consumers`, `limits`, `instruction_cites`,
    `signs` (`reviews/<year>/` or `reviews/ny/<year>/`);
  - `input_quotes.json`, with the year in a parameter's key (`input-quote:2025:ctc.threshold ...`) moved to the new
    year. This was not in the brief: the rehearsal showed 6 new input-quote findings without it;
  - `twins.json`, byte for byte (the coordinator's addition, BUG-0652);
  - the input and adapter contract banks, `tools/transcription/{input,adapter}_contracts/[ny/]<year>/*.json`, with
    `tax_year` moved. An input bank keeps its source snapshot (`text_sha256`), so if the new edition's text differs,
    the bank is refused as stale until its cases are reviewed.
- `tools/baseline_check.py` (shared by the coverage checks and the input-quote check): a baseline entry also holds when
  the subject matches the recorded hash after the two marks adoption moves are moved back one year. The marks are a
  citation's `tax_year` and a "(<year>)" edition. Without this rule, every carried line or form baseline entry came
  due: canonical cites carry `tax_year`, and the forms carry `tax_year` too. In the rehearsal all 19 operand and 5
  sign baselines came due. Any other change still makes an entry due. The match reaches back one year only.
- `tools/transcription/input_contracts.py`, `adapter_contracts.py`: `REQUIRED` used to be keyed by year
  (`{"2025": {...}}`), so 2026 silently required no bank. It is now the first year each bank is required, so a
  required bank is required in every later year (`required(year)`).
- `tools/transcription/year_carry.py --write` now writes each carried form's rulings sidecar
  (`write_sidecar`). It holds the earlier year's entries, unchanged, for the rulings the carried log shows, and
  carries `carried_from` in its header. A line shown from a renamed record section keeps its rename `carried`, and
  the year carry goes in `year_carried` (`year_marked`). Without this, `review_store.py check` reported 40
  problems on `clcw_next_year` class M.
- READMEs: `tools/README.md` (year operations section) and `tools/transcription/README.md` (carrying across a tax year).

## Follow-up: BUG-0648 fixed (render logs with no line subjects)

- `year_carry.py --write` now records a year-carried whole-form subject for a render index with no line subjects.
  The new functions are `whole_carried` and `renderings_carry`, called from `write`. The index records it as
  `subject_carried` with `carried: {by: year, from}`. It is written only when all of these hold:
  - every compared line carries;
  - the 2025 index answered the 2025 rendering;

    document's cited sources moved it. This is the rule `rest_carries` already applies to a transcription
    document's own fields.

  `recorded_subject` already reads it.

  anything else, when the sources moved no year, or when the sources kept the year in the same words.

- `test_rename_carry.py` passes, and `review_store.py check` passes on 2025.
