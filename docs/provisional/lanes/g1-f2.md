## Per bug

**BUG-0447 (medium): real; fixed.** Evidence: the `f2441.care` source read "so that you (and your spouse) could
work", the bank prompt and tick "so that you could work"; i2441 p. 2 item 2 says "so you (or your spouse if filing
jointly) could work", and a student or disabled spouse is deemed to work (i2441 p. 4; p503 p. 4, which limits the
rule for the filer to a joint return). Who could answer No wrongly: a married filer whose care let one spouse work
while the other studied or could not care for themselves, and a couple paying for a disabled spouse's care (the old
tick said "a child or a dependent"; a spouse is no dependent). For them the right end is the refusal
`f2441.student_disabled`; a wrong No gave a computed return without the credit (and skipped New York line 64).

- Source: "so that you (or, on a joint return, your spouse) could work or look for work? On a joint return, this
  includes care that let one of you work or look for work while the other was a full-time student or was not able to
  care for themselves." Cite adds i2441 p. 2 item 2.
- Prompt: "... so that you (or, on a joint return, your spouse) could work or look for work?" A couple reads "you or
  your spouse"; anyone else reads "you". Rejected alternative: "so you could work or study", which would draw a
  single full-time student who does not work to a Yes that only refuses (deemed income for the filer applies only on
  a joint return, p503 p. 4, while the tool refuses `f2441.student` for every status).
- Tick: "Care for a child or disabled adult, during work or job-search hours" (12 words). It names no one's work, so
  either spouse's counts, and names an adult. Over-Yes costs a few questions (a couple with one spouse at home and
  not studying gets Form 2441 with line 5 zero: the right value).
- Help (about 48 words, over the ~40 aim): adds "On a joint return, so does care that let one of you work while the
  other studied full time or could not care for themselves."; "whoever paid the bill" shortened to "whoever paid",
  "If you are not sure" to "If unsure".
- Coverage row `care.joint_spouse_student`: a couple, care Yes, a full-time student spouse, ends refused at
  `f2441.student_disabled`.
