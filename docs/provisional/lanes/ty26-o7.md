## Decisions

- **The minimum deduction (C-SCHED-31).** The form governs. Line 16 is $400 when the active QBI is at least $1,000
  and zero otherwise; line 17 is the greater of lines 15 and 16. This always gives the same amount as the i8995
  "Line 17" text, so the open question in the change map needs no further choice. The statute agrees: section
  199A(i)(1) is "the greater of" the deduction and $400, with no taxable-income limit.
- **Active QBI is line 2.** The code counts every Schedule C business on a completed return as one in which the filer
  materially participates. A Line G No is refused, and the passive and rental cases are refused too. The exception
  is a directly held oil or gas working interest, where line G says Yes without material participation. That case
  is believed empty because the business would claim depletion, which the Schedule C screen refuses. This is an
  inference, not a check, so it is recorded as BUG-0654. The other choice was a new screen question; it
  was not taken to avoid a question for a near-empty case.
- **The 8995-A path.** Business income above the threshold is refused, so this path has dividends only, and its
  minimum deduction is zero.
- **Section 68 on Form 8960 (C-SCHED-27): refused, not encoded.** The worksheet cuts 9a and 9b only when taxable
  income plus itemized deductions is above the start of the 37% bracket ($384,350 MFS, $640,600 single and HoH,
  $768,700 MFJ and QSS). C-PRI-18 already refuses every such itemizer on Form 1040 line 15:
  `f1040.gate.itemized_limit` screens line 11b minus 13a and 13b at $384,350, the lowest of the three. So today the
  new refusal never refuses a return on its own. It is there so Form 8960 stays right once the Schedule A worksheet
  replaces that screen. It refuses on line 9d, so the refusal list of 9a and 9b is left alone.
- **Line 8j (C-SCHED-28): no new question.** The new NII item on line 8j is rent below fair rental value.
  `scope.rental` already refuses it on every return ("rent out, or offer for rent, anything you own, such as a house,
  a room, ..."). The other hobby income on 8j is not investment income. So line 7 stays zero, and its citation now
  says why. `s1.niit.yaml` and `f1040.qbi.yaml` were not needed and were not created. The f1040 QBI line is handled by
  C-PRI-02 (printed 13b; its citation was set there).
- **C-SCHED-33.** For an itemizer, `sa.deduction` is `sa.line17`: the 2026 line 18 "No" branch, the sum of lines 4
  to 17z before any section 68 limit. That is exactly the i8995 list, so the deduction keeps reading the unlimited sum.
  The label records this so a later encoding of the limit does not swap in the limited amount.
- **Citations left to relocation.** The generic Part IV citations of f8995a lines 27 to 39 ("p. 2, Part IV, lines
  27–40") and the "Form 8995 (2025), lines 1–17" citations of the copy lines are left to `citations.py --relocate`
  and the year literals. Only the citations whose line numbers or quotes change were set.
