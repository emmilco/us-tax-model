## What was built

- `tools/bug_status.py` (new). Derives each entry's status from the inventory and `bug_pending/`: open, fixed (its own
  fields), closed by a later entry (with a kind: fixed, won't fix, superseded, duplicate, not a defect, disposed), or a
  closure record. The rules are in the docstring. In brief:
  - Own fields: `Status` / `Disposition` / `Proposed disposition` starting "fixed": fixed, otherwise open. A fix field
    (`Fix`, `Fix (owner)`, `Tool change`, `Wording fix`, `Changed form lines`) not starting "open", "not fixed",
    "either" and the like: fixed. Proposal-only fields or no field: open.
  - Closures: a `Closes:` field (its leading id list; "in part" and similar make it partial), a `Closure:` kind
    field, a `Reopens:` field. The legacy title conventions are also read ("BUG-x is fixed", "Fix for BUG-x",
    "Disposition of BUG-x", "(closes BUG-x)", "... (BUG-x) ... fixed"); "mostly", "partly", "is fixed for ..." and
    "'s ..." are partial.
  - Coverage: an open entry is covered when a `TASKS.md` row names it, or when its status or fix field names a T-id
    that is in `TASKS.md`. A `TASKS-DONE.md` row naming it is shown as a hint.
  - Modes: `--write`, `--check`, `--open`, `<ids>`, `--self-test`. The self-test includes a planted check that
    assigning ids keeps the report current, and a mutation of it (cutting titles to a width) made it fail.
- `reports/bug_status.md`, generated: 99 open entries, 13 covered by a `TASKS.md` row and 86 by none. Pending entries
  are listed last, in `--assign` order, and no cell is cut to a width, so `new_bug.py --assign` rewriting placeholders
  keeps the report current.
- Check-all: step `bug_status` (`--check`) in `docs_steps`. `tools/docs_paths.txt` gains `reports/bug_status.md`. The
  self-test row is `bug_status_self_test` (bookkeeping). There are seven `READERS` rows in `tools/tier_paths.py`.
  `tools/README.md` documents the tool.
- How D8's rule was implemented: "fail on an open entry that neither a TASKS row nor the report names" comes down to
  "the report is current", because the report is generated and lists every open entry. `--check` also fails on a
  closure that names no entry, names itself, or gives an unknown kind. Closures may come before their target, because
  `--assign` numbers pending entries by name, not by when they were written.
