## How to continue this lane (for a successor)

Everything the lane builds comes from one table, `ITEMS` in `docs/provisional/lanes/l9_work/gen.py`. Running

    python3 docs/provisional/lanes/l9_work/gen.py

rewrites, always from the files as they were at commit `100b132c` (the branch start): `forms/2025/scope.yaml` (the
item inputs, their gate lines, and the three old ids as derived lines), the `scope.*` entries of
`interview/2025/questions.yaml`, the `scope.*` defaults, the question lists of `interview/2025/sales.yaml` and
`stage4.yaml`, and every fixture's inputs (through `l9_work/refix.py`; the per-case choices are in `gen.py`, `cases`).
So: change a wording, an order, a gate or an item in the table, run `gen.py`, then `tools/draft_check.sh --quick`.
Files edited by hand and not regenerated: `interview/2025/coverage/scope.yaml`, the personas, `refusal_notes.yaml`,
`gate_frequencies.yaml`, the engine test sources, this file. If another lane's commit changes one of the generated
files, move `BASE` in `gen.py` forward to a commit that has it (the generator replaces only the `scope` parts).

## The technique (MAP 4.1): the old id as a derived line

`scope.schedule1`, `scope.other_income` and `scope.other_line16` are no longer inputs. Each is a `test` line, `any` of
"item = true" over its items; an item beneath a parent is read through its gate line (`scope.<item>_if_<parent>`, a
`choose` on the parent, false when the parent is No, the item marked `gated_by`). No reader in any other form file was
edited: every `refuses: when: scope.schedule1` (and so on) stands as it was, with the same gate, form and remedy.

The evaluator's `any` (engine/src/eval.ts, `evalTest`) returns true as soon as one operand is known true, whatever
else is unanswered, so a Yes to one item refuses at once; with no Yes it stays unknown until every item is answered.

**RAN (proof on one item, `scope.rental`, with the rest of the screen held in a temporary input):** `npm run build`
"build OK"; `interview-rules` "0 problem(s); 177 walks"; `interview-coverage` walked all 79 existing rows and failed
only `credits.partnership_credit`, which answered the old id (rewritten to `scope.k1: true`). So the technique works.

Three things a successor or the coordinator should know about it:

1. **An expected "unknown" names an input.** One fixture (`stage9.eic.refuse.other_income.schedule1_unanswered`,
   `fixtures/2025/f1040.json`) leaves the Schedule 1 screen unanswered and expects `f1040.line27a` to be unknown with
   `"input": "scope.schedule1"`. With the screen derived, the missing input is one of its items. See "Expected values"
   below for what was done.
2. **Fixtures that left a screen unanswered (`null`)** now leave that screen's own items unanswered; the plain parents
   shared by items of several screens keep their default (No), so the other screens are not made unknown by it.
3. **A fixture that answered a screen Yes** gets one item Yes (`gen.py`, `cases`): the item its evidence note names
   where it names one (clergy, Medicaid waiver, nonresident, chapter 11, Form 8814, partnership business income, farm),
   otherwise `scope.rental`, `scope.chapter11` or `scope.f8814` (the model-written class M and mutation cases state no facts).

## THE FINDING THE COORDINATOR NEEDS FIRST: an unanswered screen hides what stands behind it

**RAN (seen in the coverage walks, read in `engine/src/eval.ts`):** a line's refusal gates are evaluated before its
expression, and while a gate is *unanswered* the expression is not evaluated, so the inputs it reads are not yet
reachable and are not asked. (The gates design says a *false* gate hides nothing; an unanswered one does.) Other
forms' lines carry `refuses: when: scope.schedule1` (and the other two): `f1040.line8`, `f1040.line10`, `f1040.line16`,
`ssb.line6`, two Schedule 8812 worksheet lines, two Schedule 1-A lines, four EIC lines. A derived screen is unanswered
until **every** item of it is answered (unless one is Yes). So every question whose only way to a target runs through
one of those lines waits for the *last* item of the screen, wherever the bank's order puts it. Before, the screens
were questions 3 to 5, so nothing waited.

What it does here: four existing credits rows answered `person[taxpayer].plan` and `sc[shop].hi_uses` before a
business refusal; those two questions (reached through Schedule 1 adjustments, `f1040.line10`) are now not asked until
the last Schedule 1 item (order 295) is answered, and the rows' answers to them were removed as stale
(`credits.business_credit`, `.employment_credit`, `.business_fuel`, `.elective_payment`).

What it will do after the merge unless something is done: the questions of other topics that are reached only
through Form 1040 lines 8, 10 or 16 (other income at 160, the IRA and plan deductions at 180 and 259, student loan
interest at 240, and whatever is asked only once the tax is figured) arrive after order 295, away from their topics.
The business-gated items make it worse: `scope.se_plan_other` (a Schedule 1 item) cannot be answered before the
business list (250).

Three ways out, none of them this lane's to take alone:
1. **The engine (the order agent, T-284 cause 2):** let a line's expression be explored for reachable inputs while a
   refusal gate on it is still unanswered. This is the same change as "a child is asked whatever refusal entries
   stand before its own on a line", applied to the expression as well as to later entries. It is the right fix.
2. **Direct targets (`full.yaml`, MAP 4.7):** ask for `s1.line10` and `s1.line26` (what Form 1040 lines 8 and 10
   copy) directly, as the health savings account block asks for its three results. **INFERRED, not tried.**
3. **Give up the spread for the items that gate most:** ask every Schedule 1 item before topic 140. That is the old
   screen in short questions, in one place; it is what this lane would do if neither 1 nor 2 lands, by changing the
   `order` column of the table in `gen.py` (nothing else changes).

The same engine behaviour, inside one line's own list of refusals, is why `scope.ready` was rebuilt (next section).
