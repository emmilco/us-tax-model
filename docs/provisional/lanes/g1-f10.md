## Decisions for the coordinator or owner

1. **Rebate as consideration (BUG-0515, `carloan.down`).** There are two readings. Under (d)(2)(iii)(B), a
   manufacturer's rebate applied at purchase is either "other consideration provided by the taxpayer", and so
   reduces the nonqualifying debt, or a reduction of the price, which leaves the nonqualifying debt alone. The two
   give different values when `carloan.mixed` is Yes. The sources archived here do not settle it, so the lane changed
   nothing. Options:
   - an ambiguity register entry choosing the retail contract's "total down payment", which includes the rebate;
   - choosing the price-reduction reading, which is conservative;
   - leaving it open.
