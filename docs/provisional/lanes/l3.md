The four questions, per employer with tips:

1. `tipjob.listed`: "Is the work you got these tips for on the IRS list of jobs that customarily get tips? Restaurant,
   bar and hotel staff, hairdressers and barbers, and taxi and delivery drivers are on it." (The examples are from the
   archived list, `sources/2025/tipped-occupations.txt`: codes 101 to 110, 301 to 304, 603, 802, 804.) **A No is not a
   refusal**: that employer's tips count zero and nothing more is asked about it.
2. `tipjob.other_work`: "Did you also get tips from this employer for other work that is not on that list?" Yes refuses.
3. `tipjob.charged`: "Do these tips include a service charge or automatic gratuity that the business added to the bill,
   an amount you negotiated with the customer, or tips given as tickets, meals or goods instead of money?" Yes refuses.
4. `tipjob.unlawful`: "Was any of the work you got these tips for against the law (for example, serving alcohol
   without a license the state requires), or was any of it prostitution or pornographic activity?" Yes refuses.

All three refusals are the one gate there was, `tipjob.mixed`, with its wording unchanged (map 4.1 item 3).

**Why the No to `tipjob.listed` had to stop refusing (N9).** Today `s1a.tips` itself names the list, so a person
tipped in work off the list answers it No and the return is completed without the deduction. Once the parent asks
the plain fact ("did you get tips at a job?"), that person answers Yes and lists the employer; with the old
`tipjob.qualified` a truthful No there would have refused a return that today is completed. So the occupation test
became the first child, and its No gives the same return as today's No to the parent. The situation the old No
refused (some of one employer's tips qualify and some do not) still refuses, through the three questions after it.
A filer who today lists an employer and answers `tipjob.qualified` No although none of that employer's tips
qualify is refused today and completes now with no deduction; I count that as the same facts reaching the answer
today's parent already gives them, not as a change of scope, and name it here so the owner can overrule.

`tipjob.charged` joins three things by "or" (not voluntary, negotiated, not money). They are one printed test in the
instructions' list ("paid voluntarily, not the subject of negotiation, determined by the customer", and "cash
tips"), each refuses, and none has a No a server needs to think about; three more questions seemed worse. It is the
question of mine I am least sure of.
