## Decisions and why

1. **Vehicle interest is asked per vehicle, as the business-use part, not figured.** The 2026 text says to "allocate"
   the interest without naming a method. The 2025 line 16b also asked the business part. So this creates no new
   ambiguity and keeps the same facts giving the same return.
   - Rejected: prorating by business miles (as `scveh.operating_business` does). That needs a reading not stated in
     2026, and `prorate` gives unknown when a standard-mileage vehicle has zero total miles.
   - Rejected: a per-business input. It is asked of businesses with no vehicle, and it puts the item away from where
     its subject lives (N5).
2. **The `forty` election is dropped for 2026.** The change map said this "cannot be settled from the archive", but
   `plaw-119-21` is in `sources/2026`. Its sec. 70301(b)(3) (139 Stat. 189, archive p. 119) limits the election to
   "qualified property placed in service by the taxpayer during the first taxable year ending after January 19, 2025".
   For a calendar-year individual that is 2025, and short tax years are already refused.
   - Rejected: keeping `forty` and routing it to a refusal. That is self-contained, but it offers filers an option
     the law does not give.
3. **`carloan.col_ii_input` stays an input.** A loan entry and a business's vehicle are separate collections (spec
   4.4), so it cannot read Schedule C line 16b. Its citation is left to C-SCHED-07 (lane W1, wording), which re-cites
   the same field. Setting it here would collide.
