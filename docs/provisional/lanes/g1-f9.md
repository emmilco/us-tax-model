## T-304 (1): the design and why it stops here

The design (applied, tested and reverted; text kept below):

- `household.hoh_missed_test2` = a Test 2 person (`household.any_test2_person`) and then `household.home_cost` (the
  person tested first, so the home cost is asked only when there is one).
- `household.hoh_missed` (gate) = status `single`, `household.unmarried`, and `household.test1_met` or
  `household.hoh_missed_test2`.
- A refusal on `household.hoh_fails` (so it reaches `f1040.line12e` without editing `f1040.yaml`): "Head of household
  filing status fits you ... Go back and choose head of household", citing i1040gi p. 13 ("The ones that will usually
  give you the lowest tax are listed last") and p. 14 (Check the "Head of household" box only if ...).
- Coverage rows: a single never-married parent of a young child who paid over half the home cost is refused at
  `household.hoh_missed`; the same parent who did not pay over half proceeds.

What `test-fixtures` showed with it: the six fixtures `stage8.refuse.dependent.{other_claimant, divorced_rule, married,
multiple_support, income_exception}` and `t091.kidnapped.age19` expect `f1040.line12e`, `household.hoh_fails`,
`s8812.clw_a1/a3` (and line 19) computed while a dependent of the same return is refused; with the check, a single
filer's line 12e reads the dependents' head of household tests, so those lines become the dependent's refusal. The
return was refused in any case; only lines that previously showed a number now show the refusal. Class M cases
`classm.f8880.4` and `classm.s3.4` (single filers with a dependent) become unknown (`dependent[dep_a].months`,
`absence_no_return`) because class M cases take no defaults. Under the brief, an expected value of an existing fixture
moves, so the lane stops and reports.

Cheaper alternatives considered and rejected: (a) a question asked of every single filer ("did a child or relative
live with you ..."), a guard after the typical answer (N4), with a false positive that has no way out once HOH then
fails its tests; (b) `home_cost` plus "a dependent is listed", the same dead end for a dependent who does not qualify
the filer. The precise design above is the right one if the coordinator accepts the six fixtures' line 12e values
becoming the dependent refusal (and restates the two class M cases).

The lines, as built:

```yaml
(hung under household.hoh_fails, after its cite)
      refuses:
        - when: household.hoh_missed
          form: Head of household filing status fits you
          remedy: You chose single, but your answers meet the head of household tests (you were unmarried, and you paid over
            half the cost of keeping up a home for a person who qualifies you). Head of household gives a lower tax than single
            on the same income. Go back and choose head of household.
          cite: {doc: i1040gi, locator: 'p. 13, Filing Status, "The ones that will usually give you the lowest tax are listed
            last"; p. 14, Head of Household, "Check the ''Head of household'' box only if you are unmarried (or considered
            unmarried) and either Test 1 or Test 2 applies"'}
    - household.hoh_missed_test2: all [household.any_test2_person = true, household.home_cost = true]
    - household.hoh_missed (gate): all [f1040.status = single, household.unmarried = true,
        any [household.test1_met = true, household.hoh_missed_test2 = true]]
```
