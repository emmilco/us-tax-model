## What changed

- `tools/propose_params.py`
  - D7: a parameter declared `moves_with_year: N` (a Count) is the from-year value plus N a year, recorded with
    `derived`. Where its citation can also be read in the to-year edition, the reading must agree, or the
    parameter is declined.
  - D8: `tcw.<status>.b<k>.{upper,rate,subtract}` come from the to-year rate table (the revenue procedure, read by
    `extract_printed_tables.rate_schedule`): the row at the worksheet row's rate gives the upper limit, the rate,
    and rate x lower limit - base. The rule is checked on the from-year first: every 2025 TCW value equals it. A
    to-year schedule with a different number of rows over the Tax Table boundary gets a note on each record of
    that status.
