## Decisions made (the owner may reverse them)

1. **Trump accounts (C-PEOP-04, -11): one more answer, not new inputs.** The 2026 Form 1099-R has boxes 7c (Trump
   account) and 7d (earnings on excess Trump-account contributions). Box 7d is reported only for a Trump account
   (f1099r p. 9; i1099r p. 17). The issuer instructions say "Do not check the box [7b] for a distribution from a
   Trump account". So under the 2025 wording, a filer holding a Trump-account form would answer the account question
   "plan" (the IRA/SEP/SIMPLE box is not ticked), which is a wrong answer and a wrong return.
   - What was done: `retirement.account` gains the answer `trump` ("A Trump account (the box “Trump account” is
     ticked)"). The `plan` answer now says neither box is ticked. A gate `retirement.trump` refuses first in the three
     refusal lists of a Form 1099-R (`retirement.rolled`, `retirement.taxable`, `retirement.amount_a`), so the refusal
     is told as soon as the account is answered (protocol N11, 3.5).
   - Why: this adds no new input and no new question. A separate yes/no question for box 7c on every Form 1099-R
     would cost every retiree a step for a distribution that is nearly impossible in 2026 (no distributions before the
     year the beneficiary turns 18, p590b p. 3).
   - What it depends on: the enum value `trump` (P2), and no fixture default.
   - Consequence: the brief's "new inputs (boxes 7a to 7d) with default and reason" does not apply. Boxes 7a and 7b
     are the existing `retirement.code` and `retirement.account`, relabelled. There is nothing to add at the R3 rebase.
2. **Exception 24 (C-PEOP-05): kept refused, not built.** In 2025, `retirement.early_screen` refused a qualified
   long-term care distribution only when it was made December 30 or 31. For 2026 it refuses every such distribution:
   the screen item now reads "it paid for long-term care insurance (a qualified long-term care distribution from an
   employer plan)", and the remedy text was mended to match. Building it would need the `e24` enum value, a
   joint-return condition when the coverage is the spouse's, and a per-distribution cap (the smallest of the
   premiums, 10% of the vested benefit and $2,600). It would also rest on draft text (i5329, p590b), and i1099r says
   such distributions are reportable only "for tax years after 2026" (change map doubt). The owner may prefer to
   build it.
3. **IRA amounts (C-PEOP-08): text set now, cites wait.** The labels, the question and the refusal remedies that
   show the limits are written with the 2026 figures (i8606 draft, n-25-67 final). Where a text was replaced whole,
   its year is written as 2026. The locators quoting the 2025 i1040gi worksheet and Pub. 590-A are left as they are,
   because those 2026 editions are not archived.
4. **Help-text additions for C-PEOP-11.** `person.ira_trad` help adds "and money put into a Trump account" (about 44
   words, a little over N10's 40). `person.ira_basis` help adds "(not Trump accounts)".
