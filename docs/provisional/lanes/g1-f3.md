## Per item

### BUG-0635 (was land3-adj8-01): real; fixed (BUG-0687, a closure)

Checked against the tree. On the route where Part III differs by month (`same_months` No), `b_wrong_asked` reads only
`b_wrong`. A filer with no advance payments, or with another reason to doubt column B, therefore never reaches
`b_fixed_same`. When that filer's corrected monthly amounts are the same all year, `uniform` stays No, so
`f8962.nonuniform` counts the form and line 10 is No. i8962 p. 14 tests line 10 on the correct premium, so it should
be Yes.

The fix is the entry's preferred one, with no new question. `f1095a.months_uniform` holds when the monthly entries have
column A above zero and the same in all 12 months, and column B (which the monthly questions take corrected) the same in
all 12 months. `f1095a.annual_ok` is a `choose`: `uniform` where `uniform` is Yes, else `months_uniform`. The `choose`
keeps the monthly entries from being read on the annual route. `f8962.nonuniform` counts the forms whose `annual_ok`
is No. `f1095a.uniform` is unchanged and still picks which inputs an entry is read from.

The entry's alternative was not used: it would ask `b_doubt` on the monthly route and move column C before
`b_fixed_same`, which costs steps and reorders the bank. The helper reads the corrected amounts themselves, so it covers
every reason for a correction alike.

The new regression case was predicted by hand before running. It has the facts of P17, with the printed column B at
$400 for January to June and $420 for July to December, no advance payments, and a correct premium of $400 every
month. Predicted: line 10 Yes; line 11 (a) $4,560, (b) $4,800, (c) $588, (d) $4,212, (e) $4,212, (f) 0; line 24
$4,212; line 25 0; line 26 $4,212; Schedule 3 line 9 $4,212. Result: all 11 values matched.

Not run: the same case against the pre-fix tree. Inferred from the old expression (`uniform` false on that route) that
line 10 was No there.

Generated returns: in `engine/src/generate.ts`, a monthly draw whose coverage starts in January and whose column B
changes in January has the same amounts all year. That draw now gets line 10 Yes, as the instructions require. This is
behaviour any Lean or engine comparison of generated returns will see. No fixture was affected.
