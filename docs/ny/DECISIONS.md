## Part A. Your profile: last year's IT-201 (checklist)

These confirm that your return is the target (a full-year New York City resident, New York standard deduction) and
find any line that would move it outside. For each, look at your most recent IT-201 and answer.

**A1. Filing status (item A).** Which box was marked: ① single, ② married filing jointly, ③ married filing separately,
④ head of household, ⑤ qualifying surviving spouse? Do you expect the same for 2026?
*Why:* every table and rate schedule is by status. ③ needs your spouse's federal AGI and dependents for the household
credits. A joint federal return with a spouse who was not a full-year New York resident would be refused: New York
allows either separate New York returns or a joint New York return taxing both as full-year residents, and neither is
in the target.

**A2. Item C.** Was "Can you be claimed as a dependent on another taxpayer's federal return?" marked Yes?
*Why:* Yes gives the $3,100 single standard deduction and no household or school tax credit.

**A3. Residency.** For all of 2026, will you (and a spouse on a joint return) be a resident of New York State and of
New York City (one of the five boroughs) for the whole year? (New York's test: domiciled there, or keeping a permanent
place of abode there for substantially all of the year and spending 184 days or more there; the City test is the same
with "New York City" in place of "New York State".) Do you expect to move out of the City or the State on or
before January 1, 2027? Were items D2 (Yonkers), E (days in NYC) and F (months in NYC, expected 12) as expected?
*Why:* part-year residence, Yonkers, and the "special accrual" rules for a move are refused.

**A4. Item G.** Was any special condition code entered (for example A6 for Build America Bond interest, C2, E3)?
*Why:* A6 changes the household-credit income; most others concern dates or collection and are outside the
computation.

**A5. Item H and line 36.** Any dependents listed? (Expected: none.)
*Why:* dependents change line 36, the household-credit column and bring the Empire State child credit (a dependent
under 17) and the NYC income tax elimination credit into question.

**A6. Additions and subtractions.** Was each of these zero? Answer by number those that were not:
line 20 (interest on other states' municipal bonds or tax-exempt funds), 21 (public employee 414(h) contributions),
22 (New York 529 nonqualified withdrawal), 23 (other additions, Form IT-225), 25 (taxable state refunds or the
inflation refund check), 26 (New York or federal government pension), 28 (interest on U.S. government bonds, such as
Treasury bills or savings bonds), 29 (pension and annuity exclusion), 30 (New York 529 contributions), 31 (other
subtractions, Form IT-225).

**A7. Line 33.** Was New York adjusted gross income above $107,650? (Above it, a recapture worksheet replaces the tax
table; computed either way, but it tells which fixtures matter most.)

**A8. Line 34.** Was the "Standard" box marked? (Expected yes; itemizing is outside the target.)

**A9. Credits and other taxes.** Were all of these zero? Answer by number those that were not: lines 41 (resident
credit for taxes paid elsewhere), 42 (other State credits, IT-201-ATT), 45 (other State taxes, IT-201-ATT), 51 (other
City taxes), 53 (City credits, IT-201-ATT), 54a to 54e (MCTMT), 60 (voluntary contributions), 63 (Empire State child
credit), 64 (child and dependent care), 65 (State earned income credit), 66, 67 (real property tax credit), 68 (college
tuition), 70 (City earned income credit), 70a (NYC income tax elimination credit; new for 2025), 71 (other refundable
credits), 78a (529 deposit of a refund), 81 (estimated tax penalty), 82 (other penalties).

**A10. Household and school tax credits.** Were lines 40 and 48 (household credits) zero, and lines 69 and 69a (NYC
school tax credit, fixed amount and rate reduction) nonzero? (Expected: 40 and 48 zero unless federal AGI was under
the tables' limits: State $28,000 single or $32,000 other statuses, City $12,500 single or $22,500 other statuses; 69
and 69a nonzero.)

**A11. Line 59, sales or use tax.** What kind of entry was it: 0, an amount from the chart in the instructions, or an
exact figure from Form ST-141? (Feeds question B3.)

**A12. Lines 72 to 75, payments.** Which kinds of New York withholding did you have: W-2 wages only, or also a
pension or IRA (Form 1099-R), unemployment (1099-G) or gambling (W-2G)? Any estimated payments or extension payment
(line 75)? (Feeds B2.)

**A13. Federal items that reach New York.** Do you expect, for 2026, any self-employment income (Schedule C) with net
earnings over $50,000, any federal earned income credit, or taxable IRA distributions? (Each changes a New York screen.)

## Part B. Scope choices the map raises

**B1. Line 25 versus line 4 (state and local tax refunds).** The 2025 form says line 25 is the amount "from line 4"
(taxable refunds of state and local income tax, federal Schedule 1 line 1), but the 2025 instructions for line 25
mention only New York's inflation refund check included in federal AGI. The archive cannot settle which governs.
Options: (a) refuse when federal Schedule 1 line 1 is nonzero or an inflation refund check was included in federal AGI;
(b) follow the form label (line 25 = line 4) and ask separately about an inflation check; (c) follow the instructions
only. *Suggested: (a)* (a refusal cannot give a wrong answer; the 2026 instructions may resolve it). Does either case
apply to you? (A taxable state refund arises federally only if you itemized in the year the tax was paid.)

**B2. Withholding inputs (lines 72 and 73).** The federal encoding's Form W-2 entries have no state or local boxes
(15 to 20). Options: (a) add New York State and City withholding (and wages) to each federal W-2 entry; (b) a separate
New York collection mirroring Form IT-2's W-2 records, keyed to the federal entries; (c) two totals typed once
(State withheld, City withheld). And for 1099-R, 1099-G and W-2G withholding: compute, or refuse unless zero?
*Suggested: (b) for W-2 and refuse other New York withholding unless your answer to A12 says you have it* (keeps the
federal data unchanged).

**B3. Line 59, sales or use tax.** "Do not leave blank." Options: (a) an amount you type (0 or your own figure);
(b) the chart amount from federal AGI, with a screen refusing the cases that need Form ST-141 (an item of $1,000 or
more, business purchases, wanting an exact figure); (c) both: ask whether you owe use tax, then either 0 or the chart.
*Suggested: (c).*

**B4. Additions and subtractions on the face of the form.** For each, compute from a new input, or refuse when it
applies:
- line 20, interest from other states' municipal bonds and the non-New York part of tax-exempt fund dividends
  (needs the state split of your tax-exempt interest);
- line 28, interest on U.S. government obligations (Treasury interest, Form 1099-INT box 3; fund dividends need the
  fund's statement);
- line 30, New York 529 contributions (up to $5,000, $10,000 joint) and 529 withdrawals included in federal AGI;
- lines 21, 22, 26, 29 (414(h), 529 nonqualified withdrawal, government pensions, pension exclusion).
*Suggested:* compute 28 (box-3 interest only, refusing fund dividends) and 30 if A6 shows them; refuse 20, 21, 22, 26
and 29 unless A6 shows one. Which of these do you want computed?

**B5. MCTMT (lines 54a to 54e).** Self-employment net earnings over $50,000 allocated to New York City (Zone 1) are
taxed at 0.60%. The federal encoding computes Schedule SE per person. Options: (a) refuse when the threshold is met;
(b) compute for the simple case (all business activity inside Zone 1, no partnerships) and refuse the rest.
*Suggested: (a) unless A13 says this applies to you.*

**B6. Estimated tax penalty (line 81).** The federal encoding has no estimated-tax penalty line. Options: (a) no line
81; the tool states that a penalty may be owed if line 80 is $300 or more and over 10% of the tax; (b) a screen
refusing unless your 2026 withholding and estimates were at least 100% of your 2025 New York tax (110% if your 2025
New York AGI was over $150,000, or over $75,000 married filing separately, unless you are a farmer or fisherman), which
you would type; (c) compute Form IT-2105.9 (a separate form; not suggested).
*Suggested: (a).*

**B7. School tax credit, rate reduction (line 69a), the $500,000 test.** The instructions disqualify "income" over
$500,000 but define "income" only for line 69 (federal AGI less IRA distributions), while the 69a tables run on New York
City taxable income up to $500,000. The verification notes that the definition is
stated "for purposes of determining your New York City school tax credit", with no limit to the fixed amount, which
favours reading it as federal AGI less IRA distributions for 69a too. Options: (a) refuse when either measure is over
$500,000; (b) take that reading and record it in the ambiguity register. *Suggested: (a)* (a refusal cannot be wrong;
it only matters near $500,000). Is your income anywhere near $500,000? (If not, this never
matters to your return.)

**B8. Item C (can be claimed as a dependent).** The federal question excludes the case where the person who could
claim you "is not required to file"; New York's item C prints no such exception and says to mark Yes "even if the other
taxpayer did not claim you". Options: (a) a New York question of its own; (b) reuse the federal answer and register the
difference. *Suggested: (a)* (one more yes/no question).

**B9. Earned income credits (lines 65 and 70).** New York asks whether you claimed the federal EIC "or could you have
claimed it based on your federal adjusted gross income". Options: (a) refuse when the federal EIC is more than zero or
federal AGI is under the federal EIC's income limit for your status and number of children; (b) refuse only when the
federal EIC is more than zero. *Suggested: (a).*

**B10. NYC income tax elimination credit (line 70a, Form IT-270, new for 2025).** It requires at least one dependent on
item H. Options: (a) zero with no dependents, refuse otherwise; (b) also compute it. *Suggested: (a).*

**B11. Small items, all suggested as refused or left out unless you say otherwise:** voluntary contributions (line 60,
Form IT-227); depositing a refund into a 529 (line 78a, Form IT-195); late-filing penalties and interest (line 82);
special condition codes (item G); decedent returns; personal data (name, address, school district code) is never
encoded, and the tool would print a reminder of the fields to fill. Any you want handled?

## Part C. Design questions (how New York fits the project)

**C1. Names and layout.** Phase 1 put the New York archive at `sources/ny/2025/` with its own catalog
(`tools/sources-ny-2025.json`) and document ids prefixed `ny_` (`ny_it201`, `ny_it201i`), so no federal check changes.
Choose:
- (a) **separate tree by jurisdiction**: `sources/ny/<year>/`, `forms/ny/<year>/`, `params/ny/<year>.yaml`,
  `tables/ny/<year>/`, `ambiguities/ny/<year>.yaml`, `fixtures/ny/<year>/`, `interview/ny/`, reports in
  `reports/ny/<year>/`;
- (b) **one tree, prefixed ids**: New York documents in `sources/<year>/` beside the IRS ones, forms as
  `forms/<year>/ny_it201.yaml`, parameters under a `ny:` key in `params/<year>.yaml`, tables `tables/<year>/ny_*.yaml`;
- (c) a mix (separate sources, shared forms tree).
Either way the tools that today read only `sources/<year>/` (`citations.py`, the year diff, the source-coverage checks,
`check-all`) must be taught the New York archive; (a) touches more paths, (b) touches the federal archive and its
manifest. *Suggested: (a)*, which keeps every federal check and report byte-identical.

**C2. Line ids.** New York lines as `ny_it201.line39` (document id plus printed line, as federal), with helpers
`ny_it201.<name>`? And worksheets as their own documents (`ny_tcw` for the 16 tax computation worksheets, or one
document per worksheet group)? *Suggested:* `ny_it201.lineN` and one `ny_tcw` definition instantiated per worksheet.

**C3. How a New York line reads a federal line.** Options: (a) a plain `copy` of the federal line (`f1040.line1z`) in
cents, with whole-dollar rounding applied where New York says to round; (b) a New York input asked again (no).
And for line 19: compute it by the printed arithmetic (line 17 less line 18) and check it equals federal AGI, or copy
federal AGI? *Suggested:* (a), compute line 19 by its arithmetic, and a build-time or fixture check that it equals
`f1040.line11a` after rounding.

**C4. Whole dollars.** New York says to enter whole dollars, rounding 50 cents up. Where should rounding happen:
(a) on each federal amount as it is copied (lines 1 to 18) and on each computed tax or credit line; (b) only on
computed lines, treating federal amounts as already whole; (c) rounding steps only where a New York instruction
prints them, everything else in cents? *Suggested: (a)*, with one ambiguity-register entry stating the reading.

**C5. The interview.** Options: (a) a New York profile (`interview/ny-2025.yaml`) run after the federal interview,
asking only New York questions (residency screens first, then the additions, subtractions, withholding and line 59);
(b) New York questions mixed into the federal profile. *Suggested: (a).* Should New York refusals stop the federal
return too, or only the New York one? *Suggested:* only the New York one.

**C6. Fixtures and evidence classes.** The only official New York worked examples found are the two tax-table examples
(taxable income $38,275, married filing jointly: State tax $1,773, City tax $1,292); suggested as class A. Other
fixtures would be synthetic regression cases predicted from the source before evaluation, then class M hand-work and
reviews on your machine (Phase 4). Do you want a separate fixtures file per stage (`fixtures/ny/2025/it201.json`)?
And for class C: PolicyEngine-US and OpenTaxSolver may model New York income tax, which would give an engine
differential for New York; this session did not check (no network to those projects was used). Should Phase 3 plan
for it?

**C7. Spec and schedule.** The spec (section 2.3) lists state returns as a non-goal "until the federal coverage goal is
met". Should the spec record this branch's exception (your 2026-11-04 decision) now, or at integration?

**C8. Year handling.** The New York definitions will be written for tax year 2025 and carried to 2026 with
`tools/year_diff.py` and `tools/fixtures_year.py`. Those tools read the federal catalog only. Extend them to the New
York catalog in Phase 3, or at the 2026 adoption (Phase 5)? *Suggested:* Phase 3, so the 2025 New York data is
adopted the same way as the federal.

## Part D. Settled by the source (done this way unless you object)

- **Lines 77 to 80** follow the printed arithmetic: line 77 is the overpayment less only the estimated tax penalty
  (other penalties and interest never go on line 77); if that penalty is more than the overpayment, the difference goes
  on line 80; line 80 is the tax owed plus lines 81 and 82; lines 78a through 79 add up to line 77.
- **Federal lines feeding New York lines 1 to 18** are taken by the printed labels (only line 1 is tied to federal
  line 1z by the instructions); the verification found the mapping complete, with every component of federal AGI on an
  IT-201 line.
- **Household credit tables 3 and 6** (married filing separately) and every other table are pinned cell by cell as
  printed, not derived.
- **Tax rate schedule bases and the recapture amounts** are pinned as printed (they are roundings of exact running
  totals, so recomputing them would differ by a dollar in one place).
