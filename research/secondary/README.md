# Secondary interpretation research

Non-authoritative corroboration under `docs/2026-10-24_validation_policy.md` and spec 6.1.
These are research records, not fixture evidence or official source-manifest entries.

Each record identifies a precise reading and competing alternatives, affected lines, tax year and
facts, archived primary anchors, publisher/author, URL, observed publication/update information,
retrieval date, short excerpt and hash, upstream source lineage, contrary material, conclusion and
uncertainty. Preserve contrary evidence. Record the actual scope a source covers; silence about a
filing status or exception does not corroborate it. Separate law-change dates from publication dates.

Seek professional explanations with cited reasoning, software implementation notes and concrete
practitioner examples. Anonymous posts are leads; syndicated repetitions share a lineage. If the
official text is silent, soft evidence may support an explicitly recorded ambiguity choice. If it
contradicts explicit instructions, investigate and record the conflict. Neither popularity nor an
uncited model synthesis settles the rule.

Do not supply these materials to the existing blind source-only transcription/M requests. They
may inform research and adjudication, whose rationale must disclose that use. A commentary example
can motivate a new blind calculation case, but the independent worker must derive its expected
result from the official source. Commentary alone never becomes A/B/C/M/H/E, a noise exception or
a scoreboard assertion.

`2025-standard-deduction.md` is the first example. It corroborates two dollar amounts and explains
the limits of that support; it does not erase the owner's presentation findings.
