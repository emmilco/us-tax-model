You are giving an independent second reading of three US federal tax-year-2025 source interpretations. Use only the quoted IRS text below; answer each question with AGREE or DISAGREE plus at most four sentences of reasoning, and name any case the proposed rule gets wrong.

Q1 (Form 4952). Instructions for Form 4952 (2025), p. 3, Who Must File: "If you are an individual, an estate, or a trust, you must file Form 4952 to claim a deduction for your investment interest expense. Exception. You don't have to file Form 4952 if all of the following apply. - Your investment income from interest and ordinary dividends minus any qualified dividends is more than your investment interest expense. - You don't have any other deductible investment expenses. - You don't have any carryover of disallowed investment interest expense from 2024." p. 3-4, Line 4g: "you can elect to include part or all of these amounts in investment income ... To make the election, enter on line 4g the amount you elect to include in investment income". p. 4 Note: "The amount on line 4g is generally treated as being attributable first to net capital gain ... However, you can treat less of the amount on line 4e as attributable to line 4g and more to line 4b." Schedule D Tax Worksheet line 3 is Form 4952 line 4g, line 4 is Form 4952 line 4e, line 5 = line 3 minus line 4 (not below zero), line 8 = smaller of lines 3 and 4. Form 4952 line 8 (the deduction) = smaller of line 3 (line 1 interest plus line 2 carryforward) and line 6 (net investment income).
Proposed rule: a tax program asks two screening questions, (a) "is any interest attributable to royalties, a business, passive activities, tax-exempt income or at-risk limits, or do you have other deductible investment expenses or other investment income beyond interest, dividends and securities sales?" and (b) "do you elect the alternative allocation of line 4g?". Previously a Yes to either refused the whole return. Proposed: the two screens apply only when Form 4952 is used, meaning line 1 investment interest > 0, or a 2024 carryforward, or an amount on line 4g. When none of those exist, Form 4952 line 8 and line 4g are treated as zero and the answers are ignored. Is anything on Form 1040 for 2025 affected by those two answers when there is no investment interest, no 2024 carryforward and nothing on line 4g?

Q2 (Form 5695 Part II joint occupancy). Instructions for Form 5695 (2025), p. 3: "Residential energy property expenditures are costs of new qualified energy property that is installed on or in connection with a home that you used during 2025 located in the United States." p. 3, Joint occupancy: "If you occupied your home jointly with someone, each occupant must complete their own Form 5695. ... If the aggregate credit that would be allowed to all occupants for any item of property exceeds the credit limit below, then you must figure your allocable share of the credit from each property." p. 6, Line 21c: "Enter the full address of each home where you installed qualified energy property." p. 5: qualified energy efficiency improvements (Section A) must be for your main home.
Proposed rule: the joint-occupancy question asks about any home for which Part II costs are entered (the main home and any other home, such as a second home or a rented home, where Section B qualified energy property was installed), not only "the home" (main home). Is the joint occupancy rule correctly read as applying to a second home where Section B property was installed?

Q3 (state income tax refund). Pub. 525 (2025), p. 24, Recovery for 2 or more years: "If you receive a refund or other recovery that is for amounts you paid in 2 or more separate years, you must allocate, on a pro rata basis, the recovered amount between the years in which you paid it. This allocation is necessary to determine the amount of recovery from any earlier years and to determine the amount, if any, of your allowable deduction for this item for the current year." Example 28 covers a fourth 2024 estimated payment made in January 2025. Instructions for Form 1040 (2025), line 1 of Schedule 1, list exceptions that send the filer to Pub. 525, including "5. You made your last payment of 2024 estimated state or local income tax in 2025".
Proposed rule: a 2025 refund of 2024 state income tax is also sent to Pub. 525 (the program refuses) when any of the 2024 tax was paid before 2024, for example a 2024 estimated payment made in December 2023, because the refund is then for amounts paid in two separate years (2023 and 2024). Is this reading correct?
