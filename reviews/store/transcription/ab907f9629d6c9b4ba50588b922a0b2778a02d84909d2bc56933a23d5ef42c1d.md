    note: >-
      Open point: under method 4 of "Determining the amount of qualified tips received by an
      employee," column (c) holds the qualified tips included for this employer in Form 4137,
      column 1(c). The closed interface for a tipjob entry has no input for that amount: its inputs
      are listed, other_work, charged, unlawful and tips. The Form 4137 collection lists only line 4.
      Its per-person aggregates (person.f4137_col_c, person.f4137_filed) are fields of the parent
      collection, and a tipjob entry cannot read them. They also are not broken out by employer.
      So the supplied interface does not determine a per-employer Form 4137 column 1(c) amount.
      Chosen reading: this column is zero for each employer row, which is the IRS result when no
      Form 4137 is filed for this employer. Unreported tips on Form 4137 are then not counted in
      this worksheet's column (d) for this employer. This is a limitation of the interface, not a
      reading of the IRS text. A per-employer Form 4137 column 1(c) input, or a refusal gate bound
      at entry level, would be needed to handle that case.
