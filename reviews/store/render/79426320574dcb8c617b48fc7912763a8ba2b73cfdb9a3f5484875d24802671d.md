1b — Type of property
Status: **MATCH**

2 (rental.days_fair) — Fair rental days
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Pub. 527 says a day rented at a fair price counts as rental use even if you used the unit that day, and a day 'available for rent but not actually rented isn't a day of rental use.'

<!-- part section 2 (rental.days_personal) -->
2 (rental.days_personal) — Personal use days
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The list follows the i1040se line 2 definition of a day of personal use and its exclusions: relative renting as main home at fair rent, shared equity financing, days spent repairing, and main-home days before or after a 12-month rental.

<!-- part section 5 -->
5 — Advertising
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Advertising is a direct rental expense. Pub. 527 Worksheet 5-1 line 2d lists advertising among expenses 'directly related only to the rental activity', so it is not divided for personal use.

<!-- part section 7 -->
7 — Cleaning and maintenance
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Cleaning and maintenance is reduced by rental days over total days of use, as Pub. 527 requires. Total days are fair rental days plus personal days not also rented.

<!-- part section 8 -->
8 — Commissions
