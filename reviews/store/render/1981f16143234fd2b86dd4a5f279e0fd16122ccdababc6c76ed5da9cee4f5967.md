1b — Type of property
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The choices follow Schedule E's Type of Property codes 1–5, 7 and 8. Royalties (code 6) is left out, which is right for a rental property. Self-rental and Other are refused further down.

<!-- part section 2 (rental.days_fair) -->
2 (rental.days_fair) — Fair rental days
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Agrees with 'number of days in the year each property was rented at fair rental value.' Pub. 527 says a day rented at a fair price counts as rental use even if you also used the unit that day, and a day available but not rented does not count.

<!-- part section 2 (rental.days_personal) -->
2 (rental.days_personal) — Personal use days
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Agrees with the Schedule E line 2 definition of a day of personal use. It keeps the same exclusions: a relative paying fair rent who uses it as a main home, shared equity financing, days spent repairing and maintaining, and the 12-consecutive-month main-home rule.

<!-- part section 5 -->
5 — Advertising
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Advertising for renters relates only to the rental. Pub. 527 says 'You don't have to divide the expenses that belong only to the rental part' and lists advertising as a direct rental expense, so not dividing it is consistent.

<!-- part section 7 -->
7 — Cleaning and maintenance
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Uses rental days over total days of use, as Pub. 527 requires. A day rented at a fair price that you also used counts once, as a rental day (the cottage example: 85/99). The division applies only when there was personal use.

<!-- part section 8 -->
8 — Commissions
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Commissions to an agent who found a renter relate only to the rental, and Pub. 527 says such expenses need not be divided.

<!-- part section 9 -->
9 — Insurance
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Insurance counts only the 2025 coverage share, as Pub. 527 requires for prepaid premiums. It is divided by rental days over total days of use when there was personal use.

<!-- part section 10 -->
10 — Legal and other professional fees
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Legal and professional fees, including tax preparation for the rental, as the line 10 instructions say. Divided for personal use as Pub. 527 requires.

<!-- part section 11 -->
11 — Management fees
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Management fees, divided by rental days over total days of use when there was personal use.

<!-- part section 12 -->
12 — Mortgage interest paid to banks, etc.
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Uses mortgage interest paid to banks and other institutions as shown on Form 1098. Interest above the Form 1098 amount, which needs an attached statement, is refused as the instructions require. Divided for personal use as Pub. 527 requires.

<!-- part section 13 -->
13 — Other interest
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Other interest includes mortgage interest with no Form 1098 in your name, which the line 12 and 13 instructions send to line 13. Prepaid interest and points are limited to the 2025 share. Divided for personal use.

<!-- part section 14 -->
14 — Repairs
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Repairs but not improvements, as the line 14 instructions say. Divided for personal use.

<!-- part section 15 -->
15 — Supplies
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Supplies, divided by rental days over total days of use when there was personal use.

<!-- part section 16 -->
16 — Taxes
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Property and other taxes, leaving out local benefit charges as Pub. 527 requires. Divided for personal use.

<!-- part section 17 -->
17 — Utilities
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Utilities, divided by rental days over total days of use when there was personal use.

<!-- part section 19 -->
19 — Other
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Other ordinary and necessary expenses not reported on lines 5–18. The cost of a leasehold, which is spread over the lease term, is refused. Divided for personal use.

<!-- part section 18 -->
18 — Depreciation expense or depletion
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Depreciation of buildings and improvements, taken from the Table A-6 or A-7a rate times basis. It uses the rental portion when there was personal use. Depletion is refused.

<!-- part section 20 -->
20 — Total expenses. Add lines 5 through 19
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Adds lines 5 through 19 as the form says. Line 6 is left out, but auto and travel expenses are refused.

<!-- part section 3 -->
3 — Rents received
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Reports zero when the unit was used as a home and rented fewer than 15 days, as the line 2 instructions say ('do not report the rental income'). The home test uses personal use above the greater of 14 days or 10% of rental days, with overlap days counted as personal. Otherwise it reports rents received, including the value of property or services received instead of money.
