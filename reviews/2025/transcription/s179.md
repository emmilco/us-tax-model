The project follows the line 1 instruction on the same page ('Generally, the maximum section 179 expense deduction is $2,500,000'), for which the worksheet is offered as an optional aid ('You can use Worksheet 1'). Research from the archived 26 U.S.C. 179(b)(3)(B) and Treas. Reg. 1.179-3(b)-(c) (ambiguities/research/amb.2025.s179_carryover_no_property.md) shows the two agree on every return without a carryover, because an election never exceeds the cost, and that on a return with one the worksheet's cap departs from the statute, which measures a carryover against the dollar limitation.
