Second transcription's note: The instructions say to subtract the amount repaid of a 2025 overpayment from the total received and enter the result on line 7. The total received is box 1 of Form(s) 1099-G, or the amount actually paid if box 1 is wrong. They do not say to enter zero when the result is zero or less, so this uses a signed subtraction with no floor. The tool does not compute two cases, so line 7 is refused for them: reducing line 7 by contributions to a governmental unemployment compensation program, and repaying in 2025 compensation included in income in an earlier year.

Ruling: equivalent within valid inputs; project retained. The amount repaid is part of a 2025 overpayment of the compensation received, so it never exceeds it and the floor at zero does not apply; with no unemployment compensation both give zero, and the project's guard keeps a filer without it from being asked about repayments.
