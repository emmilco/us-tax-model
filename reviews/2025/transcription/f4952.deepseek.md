Ruling: model error; f4952 (2025) p.3 Line4d is excess total investment-property gains over total losses, including short-term gains; Line4e separately defines net capital gain (net long-term gain less net short-term loss). The second uses the net-capital-gain concept of4e in4d. This is a hand counterexample to the disputed formula, not an independent fixture or human worked-value assertion.
