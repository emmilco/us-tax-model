Second transcription's note: Who Must File: the form is needed to claim a deduction unless all three exception conditions hold. Those conditions are (1) interest and ordinary dividends minus qualified dividends exceed investment interest expense; (2) there are no other deductible investment expenses; and (3) there is no carryover from 2024. Read as follows: the form is required when there is a carryover, or when there is 2025 investment interest to claim and condition (1) fails. In scope, line 4c equals interest plus ordinary dividends minus qualified dividends. The text does not say whether the form is 'required' when there is no investment interest to claim; this transcription chose false. Condition (2) cannot be decided from the interface, so the line refuses when f4952.unsupported is true.

Ruling: model error; f4952 p.4 Line4g says to make the election by entering the elected amount on line4g. A positive declared election therefore needs this form even if the interest-expense deduction otherwise meets the p.3 filing exception. Both returned predicates omit the positive-line4g condition. For investment interest $100, ordinary nonqualified investment income $200 and declared elected dividends $50, the exception can hold while the election still requires Form4952. The project asks whether the form is needed for the deduction or declared election; no new owner scope choice is made.
