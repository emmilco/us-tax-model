## Lines

### 2 — Total cost of section 179 property placed in service
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Line 2 asks for the 'total cost of all section 179 property you placed in service during the tax year', including listed property and a spouse's property. Adding each person's cost covers both spouses on a joint return. On a separate return the spouse's property would be missing, but the separate-return refusal covers the cases where the deduction is affected.

### 1 — Maximum amount
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Enter the amount from helper "The maximum section 179 deduction limitation for 2025".

Model: The restatement always enters $2,500,000. Worksheet 1 says 'Enter the smaller of line 1 [total cost of section 179 property] or line 2 [$2,500,000] here' and 'Enter the amount from line 3 here and on Form 4562, line 1'. When total cost is under $2,500,000, line 1 should be that cost, which also changes lines 5 and 11.

Ruling: project retained (owner, T-043; amb.2025.s179_carryover_no_property). The model reads Worksheet 1 (i4562 p. 4) as fixing line 1 at the smaller of the cost and $2,500,000. The line 1 instruction on the same page states the maximum as $2,500,000 and offers the worksheet as an optional aid. With line 1 at $2,500,000, lines 5, 9, 11 and 12 compute 26 U.S.C. 179(b)(3)(B) exactly; the worksheet's cap changes no return without a carryover and wrongly withholds a carryover on one (ambiguities/research/amb.2025.s179_carryover_no_property.md). Lines 5 and 11 change as the model says, by design.
