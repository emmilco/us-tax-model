Ruling: reading recorded (amb.2025.hsa_line7_months, owner accepted after Stage 13); project retained. The register entry takes every eligible month, because line 3 leaves out the additional contribution amount for every month once a married filer had family coverage at any time (i8889 p. 5, Note, "the additional contribution amount is figured on line 7 and is not included on line 3"), so counting only family months would drop it for the self-only months altogether, and "If items (1) and (2) apply to all months during 2025, enter $1,000" (p. 7). The tension the model names is the one the register records.
