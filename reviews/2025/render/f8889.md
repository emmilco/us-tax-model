Ruling: representation; project retained. The months counted are every month treated as eligible, the owner's reading amb.2025.hsa_line7_months (i8889 p. 7, Line 7 and the Additional Contribution Amount Worksheet, "$1,000 × number of months eligible"; the Note on p. 5 keeps the additional amount off line 3 for every month once a married filer had family coverage at any time). 'You or your spouse had family coverage' is in the input: the family-months question tells a joint filer to "also count a month in which your spouse had family coverage while you were both eligible individuals" (i8889 p. 4, Line 1, "If you and your spouse are considered covered by a family HDHP, you are considered covered by a family HDHP"), so the gate's family test reads both spouses' coverage.
