Status: **MISMATCH**

The model says a married filer age 55 who uses the last-month rule with self-only December 1 coverage and earlier family months should not get $5,300, quoting the line 3 Note ("If you are married and had family coverage at any time during the year, the additional contribution amount is figured on line 7 and is not included on line 3"). Under the chosen reading i8889 p. 4, Line 3, rule 2: "If the last-month rule (see Last-month rule, earlier) applies, you are considered an eligible individual for the entire year. You are treated as having the same HDHP coverage for the entire year as you had on the first day of the last month of your tax year", so that filer is treated as self-only for every month and has no family coverage for the Note; line 7 is then $0, and the $1,000 is counted once, on line 3. The English states the chosen reading.
