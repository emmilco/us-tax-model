Status: **MISMATCH**

Model: The restatement adds every W-2 box 1 amount with no exclusion. The instructions remove two kinds: 'If you earned wages while you were an inmate in a penal institution... Do not report these wages on line 1a' and pension from a nonqualified deferred compensation or nongovernmental 457 plan reported in box 1, which 'is reported on Schedule 1, line 8t.' Same reading as the earlier rounds.
