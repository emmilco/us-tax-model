### 1a — Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Add person.box1 over every confirmed person entry, within the current person when parent-bound. An unanswered roster is unknown.

Model: The sum of W-2 box 1 matches 'Enter the total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1', including a spouse's amount on a joint return. However, the IRS also says inmate wages and nonqualified or section 457 pensions in box 1 do not go on line 1a, and a married-filing-separately filer in a community property state must follow state law; the material shown does not reveal whether a screen handles these cases.

Ruling: scope; the model asks whether the cases i1040gi keeps off line 1a are screened. i1040gi p. 23, "If you earned wages while you were an inmate in a penal institution, report these amounts on Schedule 1, line 8u. Do not report these wages on line 1a". The nonqualified deferred compensation and section 457 pension sentence is on p. 24. Both are behind s1.line8_other, whose question names "a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or a nongovernmental section 457 plan (8t); wages earned while incarcerated (8u)", and Schedule 1 refuses on it. Married filing separately is refused for the whole return (s3.mfs). A married head of household filer in a community property state is refused by household.hoh_community_property. A registered domestic partner who lived in a community property state and does not file jointly is refused at line 1a itself (household.rdp_community_property, i1040gi p. 23, "A registered domestic partner in Nevada, Washington, or California must generally report half the combined community income"). Within scope, box 1 holds none of these amounts. Same reading as the round-2 ruling of this line.
