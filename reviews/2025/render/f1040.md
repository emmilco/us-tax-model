### 27a — Earned income credit (EIC)
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Enter the amount from eic helper "Earned income credit for Form 1040, line 27a (Worksheet A, line 6 when Steps 1 to 5 are passed; zero otherwise)".

Model: Several EIC tests ask only about the filer. Step 1 q2 asks 'Do you, and your spouse if filing a joint return, have a social security number', and Step 4 q2 asks 'Were you, or your spouse if filing a joint return, at least age 25 but under age 65' (q3 likewise covers the spouse's main home). The SSN, birth-date and US-home helpers omit the spouse, so a joint return can get the wrong credit.

Ruling: scope; project retained. The spouse variants of the SSN and age tests arise only on a joint return, and married filing statuses are refused (s3.married); within scope the filer's own tests are the tests. The Step 4 birth-date boundary follows the instructions since BUG-0034.

### 27c — If you do not want to claim the EIC, check here

Ruling: scope; project retained. As line 27a: the spouse's SSN arises only on a joint return, which is refused (s3.married); the filer's own choice not to claim is not asked, and the instructed checks follow the flow chart.

Model: The form box reads 'Check if your filing status is MFS or HOH and you lived apart from your spouse for the last 6 months of 2025, or you are legally separated ...'.
