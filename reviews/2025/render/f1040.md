### 1a — Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Add person.box1 over every confirmed person entry, within the current person when parent-bound. An unanswered roster is unknown.

Model: Adding W-2 box 1 for you and your spouse (on a joint return) matches "Enter the total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1. If a joint return, also include your spouse's income." However, the instructions also say inmate wages and nonqualified deferred compensation or nongovernmental 457 amounts in box 1 must not go on line 1a ("Do not report these wages on line 1a"), and the text given doesn't show whether person.box1 leaves them out.

Ruling: refusal located elsewhere; restatement accepted. Both amounts the instructions keep off line 1a are Schedule 1 line 8 items the project refuses: wages earned while an inmate (line 8u) and a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or nongovernmental section 457 plan reported in box 1 (line 8t) are named in the s1.line8_other screen ("a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or a nongovernmental section 457 plan (8t); wages earned while incarcerated (8u)"), refused on Schedule 1 line 9, which Form 1040 line 8 reads. Within scope box 1 holds neither, so line 1a is the box 1 total. i1040gi p. 23, Line 1a, "If you earned wages while you were an inmate in a penal institution, report these amounts on Schedule 1, line 8u.
