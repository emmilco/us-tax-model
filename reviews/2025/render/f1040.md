### 1z — Add lines 1a through 1h
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Enter the amount from line 1a.

Model: The IRS line says 'Add lines 1a through 1h', but the restatement enters only line 1a. No refusal on this line names the omitted lines 1b–1h, such as household wages, unreported tips, Medicaid waiver payments or Form 8919 wages.

Ruling: scope; project retained, screen widened. Lines 1b–1h are outside the slice and are refused through `scope.other_income` at Form 1040 line 8, which line 9 sums; the screen did not name the line 1b–1h items, which is BUG-0008 (fixed: the question now names wages or tips not on a Form W-2, Medicaid waiver payments, dependent care or adoption benefits and other earned income). Line 1z must become the sum of lines 1a–1h when those lines come into scope (recorded in the transcription rulings).

### 7a — Capital gain or (loss). Attach Schedule D if required
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Enter the amount from sd helper "Capital gain or limited capital loss for Form 1040 line 7a".

Model: The restatement takes Schedule D line 16 or line 21, depending on whether line 16 is a loss. The Schedule D text for lines 16 and 21 is not provided, so this cannot be checked. Nor can the case where Schedule D is not required and capital gain distributions go directly on line 7a.

Ruling: representation; project retained. The Schedule D helper carries line 16 when it is a gain and line 21 when line 16 is a loss (f1040sd p. 2, line 16: "If line 16 is a gain, enter the amount from line 16 on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 7a"; line 21: "If line 16 is a loss, enter here and on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 7a, the smaller of: the loss on line 16; or ($3,000), or if married filing separately, ($1,500)"). When Schedule D is not required (i1040gi p. 31, line 7a, Exception 1) the only Schedule D entry is line 13, so line 16 equals the Form 1099-DIV box 2a total that line 7a takes directly. No change to the data.
