### Filing Status — Single. Married filing jointly (even if only one had income). Married filing separately. H
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The filer supplies the status from the Form 1040 checkboxes, as printed. Status consistency checks are separate refusals.

### 1a — Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Add person.box1 over every confirmed person entry, within the current person when parent-bound. An unanswered roster is unknown.

Model: The restatement adds each person's own W-2 box 1, and the refusals cover only a marriage that ended and registered domestic partners. No refusal is shown for a spouse filing separately while married at year end in a community property state, yet the instructions say 'you must usually follow state law to determine what is community income and what is separate income.' No subtraction or named refusal is shown for the box 1 amounts the instructions keep off line 1a: inmate wages ('Do not report these wages on line 1a') and nonqualified or nongovernmental section 457 pensions.

Ruling: scope — i1040gi pp. 23–24, Line 1a. The two categories the reviewer keeps off line 1a are refused upstream: wages earned while incarcerated (Schedule 1 line 8u) and nonqualified or nongovernmental section 457 pensions (line 8t) are named by `s1.line8_other`, which refuses Schedule 1. A still-married filer who files separately in a community property state is refused for the whole return by `s3.mfs` on `scope.ready` (i1040gi p. 14, Married Filing Separately), so line 1a never computes that return; `household.cp_marriage_ended` already covers the marriage that ended during 2025 (BUG-0278). The reviewer judged the line's English without those return-level screens.
