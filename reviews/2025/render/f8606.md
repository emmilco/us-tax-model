### 2 — Enter your total basis in traditional IRAs
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Enter the amount from person helper "Form 8606 line 2 for this person (asked only when the person files Form 8606)".

Model: The input takes line 14 of the last Form 8606 unadjusted, but for a form 'after 2023 and before 2025' the chart says to enter 'the amount from line 14 of that Form 8606 as adjusted to include the amount from line 6 of the Line 15c Worksheet, if any.' It also omits the instruction to 'increase or decrease the amount from the chart' for returned excess contributions, divorce transfers, or a nontaxable rollover 'that wasn't previously reported on Form 8606, line 2,' and no refusal covers these.

Ruling: second construction right; fixed (BUG-0086: the screen person.ira_basis_adjusted refuses Form 8606 line 2 in these cases, and the basis question now follows the Total Basis Chart). Line 2 is the answer to person.ira_basis, which asks for "zero if this is the first year you file Form 8606; otherwise the amount from line 14 of the last Form 8606 you filed (see the Total Basis Chart in the Form 8606 instructions for a form before 2001)". The instructions ask for more: i8606 p. 9, Total Basis Chart, for a last Form 8606 for 2024, "the amount from line 14 of that Form 8606 as adjusted to include the amount from line 6 of the Line 15c Worksheet, if any"; and p. 9, Line 2, "you may need to enter an amount that is more than -0- (even if this is the first year you are required to file Form 8606) or increase or decrease the amount from the chart" after a return of excess traditional IRA contributions, a transfer incident to divorce, or a rollover of the nontaxable part of a qualified plan "that wasn’t previously reported on Form 8606, line 2". Nothing asks about or refuses these (person.ira_excess covers an excess still in the IRA, not one returned), and the interview help says "zero if you never filed Form 8606". A filer in one of these cases who follows the question enters the wrong basis on lines 2, 3 and 14 (no 2025 tax changes; a distribution is refused).
