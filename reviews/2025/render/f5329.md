### 2 — Early distributions included on line 1 that are not subject to the additional tax
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Add person helper "Exceptions without a fixed limit" and person helper "Exceptions 05, 09, 19, 22 and 23 within their limits".

Model: Pub. 590-B sets two further limits that the program's caps and its exception-amount input omit. Exception 22 'must not exceed the lesser of $10,300 for 2025 or 50% of the present value of the IRA'. Exception 23 'must not exceed the lesser of $1,000 or your total interest in the IRA minus $1,000'.

Ruling: finding (F1, EXC-LIMITS; the project's line and the question behind it, not its English); p590b p. 28, Domestic abuse victim distributions, Distribution limits: "An eligible distribution to a domestic abuse victim must not exceed the lesser of $10,300 for 2025 or 50% of the present value of the IRA"; Emergency personal expense distributions, Distribution limits: "the amount that may be treated as an emergency personal expense distribution must not exceed the lesser of $1,000 or your total interest in the IRA minus $1,000". The restatement is right about what the project does: person.x_c22 and person.x_c23 cap at $10,300 and $1,000 only, and retirement.exc_amount asks "the amount you used for that purpose" with no word of the account-value limits, which decision D4 said would be "attested in the question's wording". A domestic-abuse distribution of $8,000 from an IRA worth $12,000 is excluded in full, where the limit is $6,000 ($200 of tax omitted). See .transcription/proposed/stage21/f5329.findings.md F1.
