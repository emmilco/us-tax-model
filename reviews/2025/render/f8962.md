## Lines

### 1 — Tax family size
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Add household helper "Number of people on the return (you, and your spouse if filing jointly)" and helper "Dependents you claim".

Model: The program adds every person on the return to the claimed dependents and never removes a filer who can be claimed as a dependent. The instructions say 'If you checked the "Someone can claim you as a dependent" box, or if you are filing jointly and you checked the "Someone can claim your spouse as a dependent" box ... you or your spouse is not included in the tax family size calculation for purposes of Form 8962, line 1.' No refusal covers this case.

Ruling: scope — the dependency exclusion is enforced at the return level: sa.dependent records whether someone can claim the filer or spouse, and its refusal on sa.standardraw propagates to f1040.line12e and the whole return (i1040gi pp. 34–35). A witness with a Form 1095-A and sa.dependent true was checked on 2026-11-01 and f1040.line24 is refused. The render review does not show the return-wide refusal.
