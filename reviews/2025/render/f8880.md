### 4(a) — Certain distributions received after 2022 and before the due date (including extensions) o
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Add helper "Distributions received in 2025 that line 4 counts (the Forms 1099-R of the return, less rollovers and the excluded kinds)", helper "Distributions from years filed jointly, counted in both columns (on a joint return; none otherwise)", and helper "Your own distributions in 2023, 2024 and 2026 (before the due date) from years not filed jointly with your spouse".

Model: The IRS asks for 'the total amount of distributions you... received after 2022 and before the due date'. A filer who is not filing jointly for 2025 drops their own distributions from earlier years filed jointly: prior_joint is $0 when not filing jointly, and prior_own_a covers only years 'not filed jointly with your spouse'. Those distributions still belong in column (a).

Ruling: representation; project retained. The model read the helper label of f8880.prior_own_a ("from years not filed jointly with your spouse") as leaving out a non-joint filer's distributions from an earlier year filed jointly. The input it sums, person.sav_prior_other, is asked as all of the filer's 2023, 2024 and early-2026 distributions on a return that is not joint ("on a return that is not a joint return: all your distributions in 2023 and 2024, and in 2026 before the due date of your 2025 return"), and person.sav_prior_joint is asked on a joint return only, so on a non-joint return every distribution of the filer is in column (a). f8880 p. 2, Line 4, "Enter the total amount of distributions you, and your spouse if filing jointly, received after 2022 and before the due date of your 2025 return (including extensions)".
