## Lines

### 1 — Foreign tax credit
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Enter the amount from ftc helper "Credit is the smaller of creditable foreign tax and regular income tax; zero if no foreign tax".

Model: The election takes the smaller of total foreign tax or regular tax, and regular tax follows the line 20 instructions: 'Form 1040 ... line 16, and Schedule 2 (Form 1040), Part I, line 1z, less any tax included on line 16 from Form 4972.' The ftc.regular_tax helper adds f1040 line 16 and s2 line 1z but never subtracts Form 4972 tax, and no refusal excludes that case.

Ruling: scope; i1116 p. 25, Line 20 subtracts "any tax included on line 16 from Form 4972", but Form 4972 is refused for the whole return by scope.other_line16 (its question names Form 4972), so no Form 4972 tax is ever on line 16 and ftc.regular_tax (Form 1040 line 16 plus Schedule 2 line 1z) is the source's regular tax on every computed return. The earlier Stage 18 finding on line 1z is fixed (line 1z is now added).
