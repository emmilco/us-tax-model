### 1 — Foreign tax credit
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Enter the amount from ftc helper "Credit is the smaller of creditable foreign tax and regular income tax; zero if no foreign tax".

Model: The form says only "Foreign tax credit. Attach Form 1116 if required". None of the pages given explain the election to claim the credit without Form 1116, its dollar limits or conditions, or whether the credit is capped at Form 1040 line 16 (or that amount plus anything else). So the 'smaller of creditable foreign tax and line 16' rule and the Form 1116 refusal can't be checked against this text.

Ruling: representation; project retained (as the prior rulings). The helper is the election to claim the credit without Form 1116 (i1116 pp. 1–2, the election to claim the foreign tax credit without filing Form 1116: the credit is the smaller of the total foreign tax and the regular tax when all foreign income is passive, reported on payee statements and within the $300/$600 limit); every failed condition refuses through `ftc.needs1116`. The ftc worksheet has its own transcription and render logs, which hold those pages. No change to the data.

### 11 — Excess social security and tier 1 RRTA tax withheld
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Add person.credit over every confirmed person entry, within the current person when parent-bound. An unanswered roster is unknown.

Model: Line 11 is "Excess social security and tier 1 RRTA tax withheld". The restatement adds up person.credit for each person, but person.credit isn't defined in the glossary, and the text given has no instructions on how to work out the excess. The refusal for married filing separately is a valid narrowing, but the calculation itself can't be checked.

Ruling: representation; project retained (as the prior rulings). `person.credit` is the per-person credit of i1040gi p. 117: zero unless more than one employer and total social security wages over $176,100, otherwise the withholding above $10,918.20, refused when any one employer withheld more than the limit (Form 843, person.over) and when RRTA compensation is reported (person.rrta). The person worksheet has its own render log, where each of those lines was judged. On a joint return each spouse figures it from that spouse's own Forms W-2 and line 11 adds them (i1040gi p. 117, "If you, or your spouse if filing a joint return, had more than one employer"). No change to the data.
