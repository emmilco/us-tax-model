Status: **UNCLEAR**

Model: The IRS line reads "Excess social security and tier 1 RRTA tax withheld." The restatement sums person.credit, but that value is not defined in the glossary, and the excess-withholding computation is not in the pages given.

Ruling: representation; project retained (as the prior rulings). person.credit is the per-person credit of i1040gi p. 117: zero unless more than one employer and total social security wages over $176,100, otherwise the withholding above $10,918.20, refused when any one employer withheld more than the limit (person.over, Form 843) and when RRTA compensation is reported (person.rrta).
