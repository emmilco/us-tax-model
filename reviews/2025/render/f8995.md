Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Add person.qbi over every confirmed person entry, within the current person when parent-bound. An unanswered roster is unknown.

Model: The IRS line says 'Combine lines 1i through 1v, column (c)', which sums QBI per trade, business, or aggregation. The restatement sums person.qbi per person, and person.qbi is not defined in the glossary, so it cannot be confirmed to equal the per-business column (c) total.

Ruling: representation; restatement accepted. person.qbi is the sum over the person's Schedule C entries of sc.qbi, which is Form 8995 line 1 column (c) for that business (person.yaml, cited to i8995 pp. 2, 4); line 2 adds them over the persons on the return, which is lines 1i to 1v column (c) with one row per Schedule C, and more than five businesses are included in the line 2 total as i8995 says ('include the income and loss from those trade(s) or business(es) in the total for line 2'). Other trades or businesses are refused through qbi.unsupported.
