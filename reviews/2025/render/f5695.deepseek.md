### 32a — If the special rule for joint occupants applies, check here and attach a statement
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: helper "Did you occupy the home jointly with someone other than your spouse, each occupant completing their own Form 5695?" = true

Model: The helper adds a condition that you are 'on a joint return'; the IRS says 'If you occupied your home jointly with someone, each occupant must complete their own Form 5695.'

Ruling: model error; the restatement checks the box exactly when f5695.joint_occupancy is Yes, as i5695 p. 7, Line 32a, says: "Joint occupants of a home who are claiming the energy efficient home improvement credit must check the box on line 32a" (p. 3, Joint occupancy, "If you occupied your home jointly with someone, each occupant must complete their own Form 5695 ... These rules don't apply to married individuals filing a joint return"). The model reads the input's qualifier "with someone other than your spouse on a joint return" as requiring a joint return; it only excludes from "someone" the spouse with whom a joint return is filed, which is the source's own exception. Joint occupancy is refused on line 30, so the box is unchecked on every completed return. Same ruling as the earlier rounds; the line is unchanged.
