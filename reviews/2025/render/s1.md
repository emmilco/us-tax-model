A Form 4797 gain (line 4) is also refused from Form 1040 line 7a on by sd.other_forms.

### 21 — Student loan interest deduction
Status: **UNCLEAR**

Restated: Enter the amount from sli helper "Student loan interest deduction (Schedule 1, line 21)".

Model: The form says only 'Student loan interest deduction.' The instructions and worksheet for line 21 are not among the pages given. That leaves the eligibility gates (not married filing separately, not claimed as a dependent) and the worksheet's line 9 unverifiable.

Ruling: defect, fixed (BUG-0076). Lines 1 to 9 are the worksheet (i1040gi p. 99): line 1 capped at $2,500, lines 2 to 4 from Form 1040 line 9 less Schedule 1 lines 11 to 20, 23 and 25 (the ones out of scope refused by scope.schedule1), $85,000/$170,000 and $15,000/$30,000, ratio to three places capped at 1.000 (amb.2025.sli_ratio_places), and the Pub. 970 exception refused (sa.excluded_income). The gate differs: i1040gi p. 98 requires 'You, or your spouse if filing jointly, aren't claimed as a dependent on someone else's ... 2025 tax return', and sli.claimed asks only 'Are you claimed as a dependent on someone else's (such as your parent's) 2025 tax return?'. Case: joint return, $2,000 of interest, the spouse claimed by a parent (sa.dependent yes, sli.claimed no): Schedule 1 line 21 is $1,010 and Form 1040 lines 10 and 11a carry it, unrefused; line 12e onward refuses on sa.dependent. sli.claimed should ask 'Are you, or your spouse if filing jointly, claimed as a dependent ...'. sli.claimed now asks 'Are you, or your spouse if filing jointly, claimed as a dependent ...', as i1040gi p. 98.
