### 1 — Taxable refunds, credits, or offsets of state and local income taxes
Status: **MISMATCH**

Restated: Choose — [refund helper "Any state or local income tax refund received in 2025" = false → helper "Year parameter sales.zero"] [refund helper "Any state or local income tax refund received in 2025" = true → refund helper "Taxable part of your refund (zero when you did not itemize for 2024 or deducted general sales taxes)"]

Model: The worksheet may be used only if none of the nine Exception cases applies. The program refuses only case 1 ('a refund ... for a tax year other than 2024') and case 5 ('You made your last payment of 2024 estimated state or local income tax in 2025'). It still uses the worksheet in cases that require Pub. 525, such as 'You owed alternative minimum tax in 2024', 'You could be claimed as a dependent by someone else in 2024', 'Your 2024 state and local income tax refund is more than your 2024 state and local income tax deduction minus the amount you could have deducted as your 2024 state and local general sales taxes', the 0% capital-gain rate case, unused credits, and a refund from a joint state return when you aren't filing a joint 2025 return with the same person.

Ruling: refusal located elsewhere; project retained. Exceptions 3, 4, 6 and 7 are refused by refund.exc_tax and exceptions 8 and 9 (with a 2024 Form 1040-NR) by refund.exc_who, both on worksheet line 1 (refund.line1), which line 9 always reads when the worksheet is used; exceptions 1 and 5 by refund.not_2024 on refund.taxable; exception 2 by the Schedule 1 screen (scope.schedule1, which names other recoveries of amounts deducted or credited in an earlier year) (i1040gi pp. 88–89, Line 1, "Exception. See Itemized Deduction Recoveries in Pub. 525 instead of using the State and Local Income Tax Refund Worksheet in these instructions if any of the following applies", items 1 to 9). The worksheet's refusals are judged in the render log of `refund`.

Lines 1, 2a, 3, 7 and 9 are figured.
