## Lines

### 1 — Taxable refunds, credits, or offsets of state and local income taxes
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Zero when there is no refund. Otherwise it takes the State and Local Income Tax Refund Worksheet line 9, which is zero when the filer did not itemize for 2024 or deducted sales taxes, as the TIP says. The Pub. 525 exceptions and Form 1099-G box 8 trade or business refunds are refused.

### 7 — Unemployment compensation
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Form 1099-G box 1 unemployment compensation (or the amount actually paid) less a repaid 2025 overpayment, as the line 7 instructions direct. Contributions to a governmental unemployment program and repayments of earlier-year amounts are refused.

### 7 (box) — If you repaid a 2025 overpayment (see instructions), check here
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The box is checked when a 2025 overpayment was repaid: 'Also, check the box on line 7 and enter the amount you repaid.'

### 7 (amount repaid) — Enter amount repaid
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Enters the amount repaid in the line 7 entry space, as the instructions direct.

### 3 — Business income or (loss). Attach Schedule C
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Totals Schedule C line 31 results, the amount the Schedule C instructions say to include on Schedule 1, line 3. Losses with amounts not at risk, passive activities and losses above the excess business loss threshold (Form 461) are refused.

### 8b — Gambling
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Gambling winnings not attributable to a trade or business, including W-2G box 1, lotteries and noncash prizes at fair market value, per the line 8b instructions and Pub. 525. Professional gambling is refused.

### 8g — Alaska Permanent Fund dividends
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Alaska Permanent Fund dividends, not reduced by the energy relief payment, per Pub. 525.

### 8h — Jury duty pay
Status: **MATCH**

Model: All jury duty pay, including pay given to the employer, per the line 8h instructions and Pub. 525.

### 8i — Prizes and awards
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Prizes and awards at fair market value. It leaves out refused prizes and section 74(b) transfers, and excludes Olympic and Paralympic medals and USOC prize money (line 8m), consistent with the 8i instructions and Pub. 525.

### 8j — Activity not engaged in for profit income
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Income from an activity not engaged in for profit, such as a hobby, per Pub. 525.

### 8r — Scholarship and fellowship grants not reported on Form W-2
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Scholarship and fellowship grants not on Form W-2. For degree candidates it takes only amounts used for expenses other than tuition and course-related expenses, per the 8r instructions.

### 8z — Other income. List type and amount:
Status: **MATCH**

Model: Totals the line 8z items as other taxable income. Only RTAA payments and kickbacks are computed; recoveries, charitable recapture, education distributions and other kinds are refused.

### 2a — Alimony received
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The line enters alimony received under an agreement entered into on or before December 31, 2018, unless it was later changed to expressly apply the post-2018 rules, and zero otherwise, as the line 2a instructions direct. The refusal names what it excludes: multiple agreements (which the instructions would total), recapture (Pub. 504) and payments partly child support.
Reviewed in: pass 947d6220e8f1

### 8c — Cancellation of debt
Status: **MATCH**

Model: The line enters canceled nonbusiness debt (Form 1099-C box 2 plus debt with no Form 1099-C), and zero when no debt was canceled. This matches 'Enter any canceled debt' and Pub. 4681's 'Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8c, if the debt is a nonbusiness debt'. The refusal names the nontaxable cases it excludes: exceptions, exclusions, deductible interest, nonrecourse and joint debt, and foreclosures.
Reviewed in: pass 947d6220e8f1

### 8f — Income from Form 8889
Status: **MATCH**

Model: IRS text says 'Enter the total of the amounts from Form 8889, lines 16 and 20.' The program uses line 16 only. Line 20 can arise only from testing-period failures, qualified HSA funding distributions or deemed distributions, which are refused, so line 20 is zero in the supported cases.
