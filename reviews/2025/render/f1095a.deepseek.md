Model: The rule would count two same-state forms with partially overlapping months, but IRS says 'Enter the amount from column B of only one Form 1095-A—do not add the amounts from each form.' It also omits the December-marriage exception.

Ruling: scope — the objection is a partial same-state overlap where both forms' column B would be counted and the overlapping months added twice, and the December-2025 marriage with separate plans where column B is added across plans. The project refuses both: f8962.two_counted asks whether two counted Forms 1095-A from one state show coverage in the same month and refuses on yes (i8962 p. 15, Column (b), "Enter the amount from column B of only one Form 1095-A—do not add the amounts from each form."); f8962.marriage refuses a return married in 2025 with coverage before the first full month of marriage, which covers a December marriage, through Part V (i8962 p. 13, Table 4).
