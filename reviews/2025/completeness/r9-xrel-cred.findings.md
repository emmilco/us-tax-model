## Findings

- BUG-0793 (medium): `eic.lived_with_relative` omits stepbrothers and stepsisters and step or foster relatives of an older generation (eic#1.1). The claim's stepparent and great-relative cases are covered by the interview help; the step-sibling case is not.
- BUG-0794 (low): `f5695.line18a` does not exclude components giving structural support or a finished surface (i5695 p. 5 Caution) (f5695#1.4).
- BUG-0795 (low): income excluded under a tax treaty is neither asked nor refused (s8812#1.1). The earlier ruling's premise, that `s1.line8_other` refuses it, no longer holds.
