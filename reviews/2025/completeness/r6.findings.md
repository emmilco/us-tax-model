## Notes on the new claims

- **dependent#3, stillborn child.** p501 p. 13: "You can't claim a stillborn child as a dependent." No question asks it,
  but a stillborn child receives no SSN, ITIN or ATIN, so `dependent.tin` is No and `dependent.claimed_no_tin` refuses.
  Witness: `stage8.hoh.child.ctc` with age 0, `born_died` Yes, `tin` and `work_ssn` No refuses lines 12e, 16, 19 and 24.
  The live-birth proof belongs to the "Died" entry for a child born and died without an SSN, which the same refusal's
  remedy names as outside the tool. Only a false Yes to `tin` computes.
- **dependent#8, Form 1098-T request date.** The claim quotes i8863 p. 1 correctly; Pub. 970 p. 2 gives February 2, and
  `amb.2025.edu_1098t_date` records the owner's choice of Pub. 970. Ruled covered against that decision rather than
  wrong, because the source conflict is real. If the owner's reading were wrong, a request made on February 1 or 2 would
  answer No: `stage19.example.jack_kay` with `edu_1098t` No moves line 29 from $1,000 to $0 and Schedule 3 line 3 from
  $1,500 to $0.
- **household#1 and #2.** Both say a Test 1 or Test 2 rule is unasked; each is asked or refused on `dependent`:
  - Test 1: `parent_home_paid` carries the rest-home sentence and the part-year rule for a parent who died. Witness:
    `stage8.hoh.parent.test1` gives head of household (line 16 $14,378) with `parent_home_paid` Yes and the refusal
    `household.hoh_fails` with No.
  - Test 2: see the evidence in the rulings file. The kidnapped child is refused through `dependent.lived_half`
    (`stage8.hoh.child.ctc` with `kidnapped` Yes: lines 12e to 24 refused at `dependent[child].kidnapped`).
