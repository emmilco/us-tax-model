## Check of the BUG-0267 gate against the claims

The brief asks whether the new gate fires exactly when head of household turns on the recount, and whether anything else
changed. Before BUG-0265, Test 2 counted every entry for which `hoh_test2_lived` holds. Now it counts only those entries
without `absence_no_return`. `hoh_eligible` is unmarried and (Test 1, or home cost and `test2_persons` > 0). So the old
and new results differ exactly when the filer:

- is unmarried;
- does not meet Test 1;
- paid over half the home cost;
- has no Test 2 person without such an absence; and
- has at least one Test 2 person with one.

That is the condition of `household.hoh_absence`, term for term. `hoh_eligible` is read only by `household.hoh_fails`,
which first tests status `hoh`, so the gate cannot reach another filing status.

## Notes on the claims

- **dependent#2, hospital stay after birth (new).**
  - **Source.** p501 p. 13: "The same is true if the child lived with you more than half the year except for any required
    hospital stay following birth."
  - **Encoding.** `dependent.months` says to count absences for medical care as time lived with you. For a child born in
    2025 who meets the other conditions, it says to enter 12 "if your home was their home for more than half the time
    they were alive".
  - **Why covered.** A required hospital stay after birth is a medical-care absence, and a hospital is not the newborn's
    home, so the plain answer counts the stay.
  - **The new line does not narrow `months`.** `qr_all_year` (T-091) states the sentence because it lists its own
    absences; its separate list does not narrow `months`.
  - **Stake if a filer read it otherwise.** `stage8.hoh.child.ctc` filed single, age 0, `born_died` Yes:

    | `months` | Line 19 | Line 24 |
    |---|---|---|
    | 12 | $2,200 | $15,642 |
    | 0 | $500 | $17,342 |

    Under head of household, 0 refuses at `household.hoh_fails`.
  - **Not introduced by T-091.** The line is unchanged by T-091 (the owner's design).
- **household#1.**
  - **Kidnapped child.** The kidnapped-child rule of p501 pp. 9–10 is refused, not encoded. `hoh_test2_lived` reads
    `dependent.lived_half` directly and through `hoh_lived_half`, and `lived_half` carries the kidnapped refusal. A relative
    under 19 is also refused by `dependent.qr_kidnapped`. The refusal reaches `test2_persons`, `test2_absence_persons` and
    `household.hoh_absence` alike.
  - **One-taxpayer limit and name entry.** Both are as ruled in round 6 and round 5 (N2).
  - **The claim's account of the new gate.** The claim says `hoh_eligible` "refuses only temporary absences that head of
    household does not count and community property". That is accurate.
