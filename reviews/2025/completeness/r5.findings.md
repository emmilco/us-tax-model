## Low (for T-092)

- **L1. The TANF note is missing from the two home-cost questions (household#1, household#2).**
  - **Source.** p501 p. 9, Note: "if you received Temporary Assistance to Needy Families (TANF) payments or other similar
    payments and used the payment to support another person, those payments are considered support you provided for
    that person".
  - **Encoding.** BUG-0260's own-funds sentence says amounts paid from funds received in the name of a person who lives
    in the home count as paid by that person. With that sentence in place, a filer whose TANF grant is issued for the
    child may answer No.
  - **Why low.** A No ends in a refusal, not a wrong amount. With `stage8.hoh.child.ctc` and `household.home_cost`
    false, lines 12e, 16 and 24 are refused at `household.hoh_fails`. With `stage21.pending1.qss_valid_computes` and
    `household.qss_eligible` false, they are refused at `household.qss_fails`.
  - **Earlier rounds.** Fix round 4 recorded the same omission as L1, and round 4 of this review treated TANF as covered
    by the natural answer. BUG-0260's sentence weakens that reading, so the claim is now ruled low rather than covered.
  - **Minimal fix.** "TANF or similar payments you received and used for the home count as paid by you."
