# Predictor A notes: Stage 19 blind returns (Form 8863)

Sources: f8863, i8863 (Part III, line 7, CLW, AQEE Worksheet), p970 ch. 2-3, i1040gi (std. deduction
$15,750 / $31,500 / $23,625; Tax Table; TCW p. 80), i1040s8812 (CLW A), f1040s8812, f1040s3.

General readings used everywhere:
- AQEE Worksheet done once per student over the whole year (facts give no per-period split).
- AOC QEE = required tuition/fees + books not required; LLC QEE = required tuition/fees only (i8863 "Qualified
  Education Expenses", Table 1).
- Line 6 / line 17 rounded to exactly three places (form: "at least three"). Cents kept, rounded half-up at each line.
- Part I lines 2-9 completed (with zeros) even when line 1 = 0 (E3, E9, E10): the form has no skip instruction keyed to
  line 1 = 0, only to line 4 <= 0. A preparer could equally leave Part I blank.
- Line 8 skipped when the line 7 box is checked; Form 1040 line 29 then reported as 0.
- Part III lines not reached after a "Stop" reported as "blank".
- CLW line 5 = 0 (no Sch 3 lines 1, 2, 6d, 6l). 8812 CLW A line 2 = Sch 3 line 3 only; CLW B not needed.
- Form 1040 line 22 = line 18 - (line 19 + Sch 3 line 8); no other taxes, so line 24 = line 22.
