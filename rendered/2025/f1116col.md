# Form 1116 (passive category) — one column of Part I and its line of Part II

- Document: `f1116col` (form, tax year 2025)
- Collection: keyed entries; [i1116] p. 11, Line i, "Use a separate column in Part I and a separate line in Part II for each country or territory"; "Total all income, in the applicable category, passed through from the mutual fund or other RIC and enter the total in a single column in Part I." (2025; printed date 2025-12-23; sha256 6998cf8c)
- Note: One entry per column of Part I (and its line of Part II) — all mutual funds and other RICs together as one "RIC" column, and one column for each country of foreign shares or bonds held directly. Income and tax are as a Form 1099-DIV, 1099-INT or a fund's year-end statement reports them in U.S. dollars ("1099 taxes"), so the foreign-currency columns are not used. Foreign qualified dividends enter line 1a unadjusted; a return that must adjust them is refused on Form 1116 line 35.
