# Form 5695 — Residential Energy Credits (Parts I and II)

- Document: `f5695` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Part I (residential clean energy credit, to Schedule 3 line 5a) and Part II (energy efficient home improvement credit, to Schedule 3 line 5b), with the Residential Clean Energy Credit Limit Worksheet (line 14) and the Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit Limit Worksheet (line 31). Fuel cell property (lines 7a to 11), business use of an item, joint occupancy with anyone other than a spouse, spouses with separate main homes, improvements related to the construction of a home and items without a valid QMID are refused. Addresses and QMIDs are not values (the data model has no strings); a QMID is an attestation per category. Each part is figured only when its screen question is answered Yes; otherwise its Schedule 3 line is zero.
