# Form 8853, Section C — long-term care insurance contracts, one per insured person

- Document: `f8853c` (form, tax year 2025)
- Collection: keyed entries; [i8853] p. 9, Filing Requirements for Section C, "Go through this chart for each insured person for whom you received long-term care (LTC) payments" (2025 (the revision for tax year 2025, archived from irs-prior after irs.gov posted a later revision); sha256 cefff141)
- Note: One entry per insured person for whom you (or, on a joint return, your spouse) received payments in 2025 under a long-term care insurance contract, or accelerated death benefits under a life insurance contract. Payments made to repay costs, and accelerated death benefits not paid at a set rate, are not taxable and need no Section C figures. Payments at a set rate (per diem or other periodic payments) are compared with $420 a day for the days of the long-term care period, or the care costs if more, less reimbursements; any excess is taxable on Schedule 1, line 8e. Payments to anyone else for the same insured, more than one long-term care period in 2025 (a change in the payment rate), a contract that is not a qualified long-term care insurance contract, and reimbursements under a contract issued before August 1, 1996, are refused. Sections A and B (Archer and Medicare Advantage MSAs) are refused by scope.archer_msa. Lines 14a, 14b (the insured's name and number) and 16 (terminally ill) change no amount and are not shown; amounts received while the insured was terminally ill are left out of line 19, as its instruction says.
