# Schedule B (Form 1116) — Foreign Tax Carryover Reconciliation Schedule (passive category)

- Document: `f1116sb` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Filed with the passive category Form 1116 when foreign tax is carried into 2025 or a carryover is generated. Line 1 is the 2024 Schedule B line 8, column by column; line 2 is empty (an adjustment is refused); line 4 uses the carryover oldest column first up to the excess limitation; line 5 is the 10th preceding year's carryover left unused; line 6 is the current year's excess foreign tax; nothing is carried back (line 7 is zero, and a return whose 2024 limit had room is refused).
