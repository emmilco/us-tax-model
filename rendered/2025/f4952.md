# Form 4952 — supported investment-interest deduction and election

- Document: `f4952` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: The election is supplied by the taxpayer, not optimized or made automatically. Capital-gain-first allocation is used. The carryforward from 2024 (2024 line 7) is entered on line 2, and the carryforward to 2026 is shown on line 7. The form is used only with investment interest, a 2024 carryforward or a line 4g election; otherwise lines 4g (as elected) and 8 are zero for Schedule A and the Schedule D Tax Worksheet, and its screens are not reached.
