# Form 1116 — Foreign Tax Credit (passive category, one form)

- Document: `f1116` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Prepared when foreign tax is owed a credit but the election without Form 1116 is not available or a carryover is claimed. One Form 1116, category c (passive), with income and tax reported in U.S. dollars on Forms 1099-DIV, 1099-INT or similar statements, and Schedule B (Form 1116) for a carryover claimed or generated. Refused on line 35 are other categories, tax paid directly, foreign capital gains, redeterminations, line 12 reductions, loss accounts, a changed or divided carryover, Schedule C income, state or local income tax among itemized deductions, the asset method for interest, adjusted qualified dividends, high-taxed income, a U.S. loss and a carryback to 2024.
