# Form 4562, Part I — Election To Expense Certain Property Under Section 179 (Summary for the return)

- Document: `s179` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Part I figured once for the return, both spouses together on a joint return (lines 2 and 11). When line 12 equals the amounts elected for 2025 each business deducts its own elections; otherwise the whole of line 12 goes to the return's only Schedule C, and more than one Schedule C is refused.
