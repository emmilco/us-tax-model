# Form 2441 — Child and Dependent Care Expenses (Parts I to III)

- Document: `f2441` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Part I (care providers, collection dcprov), Part II (the credit, with the line 10 Credit Limit Worksheet) and Part III (dependent care benefits from Form W-2 box 10). Qualifying persons are the dependent entries (dependent.dc_qp), their column (d) expenses asked per entry. Refused here or on the lines that read this form - married filing separately, line 9b (2024 expenses paid in 2025), deemed income for a student or disabled spouse or filer (and with it a disabled spouse as a qualifying person), a qualifying person who is not your dependent, a spouse who died in 2025, a qualifying person with no SSN, ITIN or ATIN, nontaxable combat pay, excluded foreign or possessions income, an ineligible related care provider, two dependent care plans with different maximums and benefits from your own sole proprietorship or partnership (sc.special, scope.schedule1). Schedule 3 line 2 and Form 1040 line 1e read lines 11 and 26.
