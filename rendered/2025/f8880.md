# Form 8880 — Credit for Qualified Retirement Savings Contributions (the saver's credit)

- Document: `f8880` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Lines 1 to 12, column (a) for you and column (b) for your spouse on a joint return, with the Credit Limit Worksheet. Each column's contributions and eligibility are this person's (person.sav_*); line 4 takes the 2025 Forms 1099-R of the return (both columns on a joint return) and each person's distributions of the other years of the testing period. Line 1 counts traditional IRA contributions for 2025, deductible or not (person.ira_contrib), with the Roth contributions. Excess or recharacterized IRA contributions, ABLE contributions and distributions, code H, year-designated box 12 entries and deferrals over the limit are refused.
