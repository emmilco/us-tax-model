# 28% Rate Gain Worksheet — distribution-only path

- Document: `g28` (form, tax year 2025)
- Note: Only 1099-DIV box 2d is supported. Direct collectibles/section 1202 sales, other source forms and carryovers are screened by Schedule D; box 2c is explicitly refused.

## 1 — `g28.line1`

- **IRS:** Enter the total of all collectibles gain or (loss) from items you reported on Form 8949, Part II
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Enter the amount from div helper "Zero only after distribution scope checks".
- Expr: `{"from":"div.screen","op":"copy"}`
- Cite: [i1040sd] p. 11, 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, lines 1–7 (2025; printed date 2025-12-11; sha256 650643a6)

## 2 — `g28.line2`

- **IRS:** Enter as a positive number the total of: any section 1202 exclusion you reported in column (g) of Form 8949, Part II, with code “Q” in column (f), that is 50% of the gain; 2/3 of any such exclusion that is 60% of the gain; and 1/3 of any such exclusion that is 75% of the gain. Don’t make an entry for any section 1202 exclusion that is 100% of the gain
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Enter the amount from div helper "Zero only after distribution scope checks".
- Expr: `{"from":"div.screen","op":"copy"}`
- Cite: [i1040sd] p. 11, 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, lines 1–7 (2025; printed date 2025-12-11; sha256 650643a6)

## 3 — `g28.line3`

- **IRS:** Enter the total of all collectibles gain or (loss) from Form 4684, line 4 (but only if Form 4684, line 15, is more than zero); Form 6252; Form 6781, Part II; and Form 8824
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Enter the amount from div helper "Zero only after distribution scope checks".
- Expr: `{"from":"div.screen","op":"copy"}`
- Cite: [i1040sd] p. 11, 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, lines 1–7 (2025; printed date 2025-12-11; sha256 650643a6)

## 5 — `g28.line5`

- **IRS:** Enter your long-term capital loss carryovers from Schedule D, line 14; and Schedule K-1 (Form 1041), box 11, code D
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Enter the amount from div helper "Zero only after distribution scope checks".
- Expr: `{"from":"div.screen","op":"copy"}`

## 4 — `g28.line4`

- **IRS:** Enter the total of any collectibles gain reported to you on: Form 1099-DIV, box 2d; Form 2439, box 1d; and Schedule K-1 from a partnership, S corporation, estate, or trust
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Enter the amount from div helper "Collectibles (28%) gain, box 2d".
- Expr: `{"from":"div.box2d","op":"copy"}`
- Cite: [i1040sd] p. 11, 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, lines 1–7 (2025; printed date 2025-12-11; sha256 650643a6)

## 6 — `g28.line6`

- **IRS:** If Schedule D, line 7, is a (loss), enter that (loss) here. Otherwise, enter -0-
- **Encoded (Dollars):** Enter the smaller of sd line 7 or sd helper "No amount on an inapplicable line".
- Expr: `{"of":["sd.line7","sd.zero"],"op":"smaller_of"}`
- Cite: [i1040sd] p. 11, 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, lines 1–7 (2025; printed date 2025-12-11; sha256 650643a6)

- **Encoded (Dollars):** Add line 1, line 2, line 3, line 4, line 5, and line 6.
- Expr: `{"of":["g28.line1","g28.line2","g28.line3","g28.line4","g28.line5","g28.line6"],"op":"sum"}`

## 7 — `g28.line7`

- **IRS:** Combine lines 1 through 6. If zero or less, enter -0-. If more than zero, also enter this amount on Schedule D, line 18
- **Encoded (NonNegDollars):** Subtract sd helper "No amount on an inapplicable line" from helper "Add g28.line1, g28.line2, g28.line3, g28.line4, g28.line5, g28.line6". If zero or less, enter -0-.
- Expr: `{"from":"g28.combined","op":"subtract_floor0","take":"sd.zero"}`
