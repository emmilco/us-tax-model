- Rulings written before stamping (T-223) carry no date. The window table counts rulings and bug entries absent at `0a43f15` (the commit before the ledger began), so the dollars sit beside the evidence they bought. A ruling edited in the window counts as new; window output not yet ruled is cost with no evidence. Stamped rulings are also joined to their calls exactly (the exact-join table).

- Noise is the model-error share of rulings (completeness: claims ruled covered or wrong). A transcription or render ruling is a disagreement to adjudicate, not a claimed defect, so the model-error share is the adjudication cost of the kind.
